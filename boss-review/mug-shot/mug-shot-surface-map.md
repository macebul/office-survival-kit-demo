# Mug Shot -- Surface Map (Complete Copy Specification)

**Last Updated:** June 23, 2026
**Purpose:** Every word of copy that goes on the Mug Shot box and mug, with exact surface placement. This document is self-contained -- no other document is needed to execute the design.
**Box dimensions:** 4.0" wide x 5.0" tall x 4.0" deep
**Box material:** 18pt paperboard, printed all 6 sides
**Box position in briefcase:** Top-left corner, visible immediately when briefcase opens
**Box features:** NO window, NO die-cut, NO insert card
**Mug:** 11oz enamel camp mug, matte black rim, printed both sides

---

## BOX FRONT (display face -- visible from briefcase top-down view)

### Element 1: Brand + Division (top of face)

```
ELEVATE CO.
Center for Drinkable Chemicals (CDC)
```

**Design note:** "ELEVATE CO." is the parent brand, always top. "Center for Drinkable Chemicals (CDC)" is the division name for this item -- styled as a department header. The acronym CDC is intentional and should be visible.

### Element 2: Product Name (hero headline -- largest text on the face)

```
MUG SHOT
```

**Design note:** Large, bold, dominant. This is the product name and should be the first thing the eye reads. Include the trademark symbol (TM) in small superscript.

### Element 3: Brad's Rebrand (directly below product name, smaller)

```
Nerve Agent Dispensary
```

**Design note:** Include the trademark symbol (TM). This is Brad's rebranding of the product -- styled as a secondary product line name. Should feel like a subtitle to MUG SHOT, not a competing headline. The v01 had "Morale Delivery System" here -- this is the updated version.

### Element 4: Primary Subtitle (below rebrand)

```
Holds your drink. Holds your composure. Holds up in court.
```

**Design note:** This is the locked subtitle. Three-part parallel structure. Should read as a confident product claim in institutional voice.

### Element 5: Contains Line (specification-style, below subtitle)

```
CONTAINS: 1 MUG -- WHATEVER'S IN IT IS YOUR BUSINESS
```

**Design note:** Styled as an institutional contents declaration. All caps. The dash separates the factual count from the editorial. The v01 had "CONTAINS: 1 NERVE AGENT DELIVERY SYSTEM | 12 FL OZ CAPACITY" -- this is the updated version. The contains and capacity lines are now split into two separate lines.

### Element 6: Capacity Line (specification-style, directly below contains line)

```
CAPACITY: 12 FL OZ / 8 SHOTS / 1 ALIBI
```

**Design note:** Mirrors the three-part structure of the subtitle above it (drink / composure / court maps to oz / shots / alibi). "8 SHOTS" plays on both espresso shots and gunshots. "1 ALIBI" escalates to absurdity while maintaining the flat institutional format.

### Element 7: Booking Photo (visual element -- lower portion of face)

**Format:** Illustration or stylized photo of the mug itself, presented as a police booking photograph.

**Visual details:**
- The mug is "posing" for a booking photo -- front-facing, centered
- A case number placard sits in front of or below the mug (like a mugshot name board)
- A large "PROCESSED" stamp is overlaid on or near the photo, angled slightly, in orange/red ink
- The booking photo replaces the bottom text line that was in v01 ("Designed for the daily lineup")

**Design note:** This visual element was added because copy alone wasn't carrying enough comedy weight at the bottom of this face. The booking photo of the mug is the joke -- the mug has been "processed" through intake. No additional text line is needed below or around the photo. The v01 had text reading "Designed for the daily lineup" in this area -- that line has been cut in favor of the visual.

## BOX BACK (Field Report)

### Element 1: Report Title (top of face, prominent)

```
FIELD REPORT
```

### Element 2: Division + Context Line (below title, smaller)

```
Center for Drinkable Chemicals (CDC) -- First Deployment
```

**Design note:** The v01 had "Processing & Intake Bureau -- First Deployment" here. Updated to CDC.

### Element 3: Subject Line (opening statement of the report)

```
Subject was issued one (1) Mug Shot Nerve Agent
Dispensary. The following was observed:
```

**Design note:** The TM symbols on "Mug Shot" and "Nerve Agent Dispensary" are optional here -- they appear on the front face. This line sets up the institutional report format. The v01 had "Mug Shot Morale Delivery System" -- updated to match new rebrand.

### Element 4: Observation Log (timestamp sequence with paired icons)

Each timestamp line has a small stick figure icon to its left showing the described behavior. Icons should be simple, expressive, and slightly playful -- like bathroom-sign-style figures acting out each scene.

```
[icon] 0800 -- Unresponsive. Quiet.
[icon] 0900 -- Making ~~promises~~ threats.
[icon] 0930 -- Chased someone with a stapler. Allegedly.
[icon] 1015 -- Stapler confiscated. Second stapler produced.
[icon] 1100 -- Building evacuated.
```

**Design notes:**

- **0800 icon:** Figure slumped at desk, head down
- **0900 icon:** Figure standing, gesturing aggressively. "Promises" is struck through with "threats" replacing it -- this must be visually clear as a strikethrough/correction
- **0930 icon:** Figure chasing another figure, arm raised with small stapler shape
- **1015 icon:** Figure with hands up (surrendering stapler), second stapler visible in other hand or behind back
- **1100 icon:** Multiple figures running out of a building shape, or building with figures streaming out

The v01 had 8 timestamp lines (0800 through 1100) with no icons. This version consolidates to 5 lines and adds stick figure icons. Removed lines from v01: "0615 -- Quiet." (combined with 0800), "0630 -- Verbal." (cut), "0715 -- Making promises." (combined into 0900 with the strikethrough gag), "1030 -- Seen leaving the building with a chair." (cut), "1045 -- Three people unaccounted for." (cut). The stapler thread (0930 + 1015) is new material replacing the cut middle lines.

### Element 5: Result Block (below the observation log, boxed or visually separated)

```
RESULT: OUTSTANDING SUCCESS
-- 100% employee engagement achieved.
-- Employee located 3 hours later.
   Described as "refreshed."
```

**Design note:** "RESULT: OUTSTANDING SUCCESS" should be prominent -- bold or in a different color (orange). The bullet points below are the punchline: the "success" metrics are absurd. "100% employee engagement achieved" connects back to the building being evacuated (everyone was engaged -- in fleeing). This section was strong in v01 and is kept as-is.

### Element 5: Brad's Quote

**Design note:** This is Personal Brand Brad voice -- completely sincere. He genuinely believes having his mug shot on file in multiple jurisdictions demonstrates leadership initiative. The quote was strong in v01 and is kept as-is. The gap between "mug shot on file" (criminal record) and "leadership is about visibility" (corporate buzzword) is the joke. ## SIDE B (Safety Data Sheet)

### Element 1: SDS Title (top, prominent)

```
SAFETY DATA SHEET
```

**Design note:** Styled as a real SDS header -- institutional, serious format.

### Element 2: Section Header

```
CONTENTS MAY CAUSE:
```

### Element 3: Effects List (with paired icons)

Each list item has a small icon to its left. Icons should be simple and expressive -- similar in style to the Field Report stick figures but can be more symbolic/abstract.

```
[icon] Opinions
[icon] Volume
[icon] Insubordination
[icon] Unsolicited strategy
[icon] A second mug
[icon] A following
```

**Icon suggestions:**
- **Opinions:** Speech bubble or megaphone
- **Volume:** Sound waves or volume knob turned to max
- **Insubordination:** Figure with arms crossed, or a broken chain
- **Unsolicited strategy:** Whiteboard or flowchart
- **A second mug:** Two mugs
- **A following:** Group of small figures trailing behind a leader figure

**Design notes:**

The v01 had two additional sections below the list:

```
IF SYMPTOMS PERSIST:
Consult no one. You have never been better.

RECOMMENDED DOSAGE:
As needed. Then more than that.
```

These sections have been **removed**. The list ends on "A following" which is the strongest punchline -- adding sections below it stepped on that joke. The icons replace the removed sections as the visual element that fills the space and prevents the face from feeling empty. This mirrors the approach used on the Lifer Ink SDS panel (icons per list item).

## TOP FACE (drinkware lineup)

### Element 1: Lineup Illustration

**Format:** Line drawing / outline illustration of 5 vessels standing side by side against a height-chart background (horizontal measurement lines behind them, like a police lineup wall).

**Vessels (left to right):**
1. Bottle (tall)
2. Disposable cup (short)
3. Coffee mug (medium) -- **circled** with an arrow pointing to it
4. Wine glass (medium-tall)
5. Martini glass (medium)

**Design note:** The mug is identified/singled out from the lineup -- circled with an orange/highlight circle and an arrow pointing down at it. The other vessels are "suspects" standing in a police lineup. The height-chart lines behind them complete the booking lineup visual. This surface was strong in v01 and is kept as-is.

### Element 2: Identification Line (below the lineup illustration)

```
"That is the one."
```

**Design note:** In quotes -- someone is identifying the mug from the lineup. Styled as a witness statement. The period makes it deadpan and certain.

## MUG FRONT (printed on the mug itself)

### Element 1: Product Name (top of mug, large)

```
MUG SHOT
```

**Design note:** Bold, prominent. Include TM in superscript.

### Element 2: Booking Height Chart (main visual -- fill level markers)

**Format:** Horizontal lines at different heights on the mug, styled as a police booking height chart. Each line is labeled with a fill level that doubles as a criminal status:

```
FLIGHT RISK          (near rim -- full)
PERSON OF INTEREST   (upper-mid -- 3/4 full)
DOUBLE SHOT          (lower-mid -- half)
SINGLE SHOT          (near bottom -- 1/4 full)
```

**Design note:** The height chart lines look exactly like the measurement lines on a booking photo backdrop, but they're also coffee fill levels. "SINGLE SHOT" and "DOUBLE SHOT" are espresso terms that escalate into criminal classifications ("PERSON OF INTEREST" then "FLIGHT RISK"). The more coffee you pour, the more dangerous you become. This was strong in v01 -- kept as-is. Note: v01 image shows "SINGLE SHIT" and "DOUBLE SHIT" which appear to be AI text-generation errors. The correct text is "SINGLE SHOT" and "DOUBLE SHOT."

### PROMPT NOTES

- These prompts are for Midjourney v6.1
- AI image generators struggle with exact text reproduction -- the generated images will approximate the text layout and feel but individual words may be garbled or misspelled. The purpose of these mockups is to validate the **layout, density, visual hierarchy, and overall feel** -- not to produce print-ready copy.
- Side A, Side B, Top, and Bottom prompts are omitted because those faces had strong v01 mockups that only need minor text updates (title changes, section removals). New mockups for those faces can be generated by adapting the front/back prompt structure with the relevant copy.
- The `--ar 4:5` aspect ratio matches the 4.0" x 5.0" box face proportions.
- The `--s 250` flag sets stylization. Adjust up for more artistic interpretation, down for more literal rendering.
- Mug prompts are omitted -- the v01 mug mockups were accurate and require no changes.
