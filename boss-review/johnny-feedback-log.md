## How to log a reply (2 minutes)

1. Paste his reply exactly as he sent it in the **Raw replies** section below, with the date.
2. Add one row per line to the table:
   - **Verdict** is one of: `LAUGHED` (any positive word) · `CLOSE` ("good premise", "needs work") · `NO` · `SILENT` (he skipped it). **SILENT counts as no.**
   - **Exact words** are his own words only, no paraphrase.
   - **Test** says which [taste-profile](johnny-taste-profile.md) test the line was betting on, and whether this confirms or kills it.
3. Any line he LAUGHED at gets status APPROVED on the item's surface map, with the date.
4. Any line he wrote or rewrote is locked as he wrote it.
