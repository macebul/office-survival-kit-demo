# Artifact 1: Basic Product Profile

**Product Name**
- From the Desk of Brad Sterling

**In-Universe Framing**
- An Elevate Co. publication
- "A Brad Sterling Publication"
- Volume I, First Edition

**Parent Product**
- Office Survival Kit (Doc Set 1 of 2)

**Brand**
- Witty Yeti (Amazon brand)
- Elevate Co. (in-universe fictional company, appears throughout)

**Product Type**
- Saddle-stitched narrative booklet with full-color editorial illustrations

**Role in Kit**
- Discovery item
- First thing the recipient sees when the briefcase lid opens
- Rests on top of Tray 1, covering all item boxes beneath
- Sets the world, establishes Brad as a character, primes the unboxing with narrative tension
- Builds anticipation so the reader wants to explore the kit

**Physical Specs**
- Format: 16-page saddle-stitched booklet (4 sheets, 2 staples on spine)
- Dimensions: ~5.5" x 7" portrait
- Cover stock: Heavier weight, matte coated
- Cover finish: Matte black with gold foil and white serif type
- Interior stock: Matte coated text weight, off-white/cream
- Color: Full color throughout (CMYK + gold foil on cover)

**Design System Summary**
- **Color palette:**
  - Matte black (#1A1A1A) for covers and occasional accent backgrounds
  - Gold (#C5A55A) for accents, rules, phase labels, Brad's name
  - Off-white/cream (#F5F0E8) for all interior page backgrounds
  - White (#FFFFFF) for text on dark backgrounds
  - Purple (#5B2C6F) used sparingly for pull quotes and subtle accents
  - Charcoal (#333333) for body text
- **Typography:**
  - Sans-serif (Montserrat/Gotham) for headers, labels, phase markers
  - Serif (Playfair Display/Freight Display) for quotes, copy, punchlines
  - Monospace (Space Mono/IBM Plex Mono) for data, fine print, technical elements
- **Image style:**
  - Editorial illustration, warm muted tones
  - Brad appears throughout in full visual DNA (purple satin, gold chain, aviators, smile)
  - Polished but with character, slightly stylized
- **Interior texture:**
  - Subtle paper texture on cream, warm and premium
  - No distressed or aged textures (that belongs to the OG Manual, not Brad's brand)

**Narrative Structure**
- Three-act structure narrated entirely in Brad's voice
- **Phase 1 - Assessment (Pages 2-5):**
  - Brad observing the reader's office world
  - Topics: Day 1 intake, the cubicle, the block, survival instincts
- **Phase 2 - Diagnosis (Pages 6-13):**
  - Brad identifies the problem (it's you)
  - Topics: The pen experiment, the daily loadout, earned ink, the office code
- **Phase 3 - Intervention (Pages 14-15):**
  - Brad arrives with the solution (himself and his kit)
  - Topics: Brad's portrait with open briefcase, the closing manifesto
- Phase labels appear as small gold design elements on the first page of each act
- They are not standalone dividers

**Discovery Context**
- Rests face-up on top of Tray 1 inside the closed briefcase
- First thing visible when the lid opens
- The recipient picks it up, reads it, and then discovers the item trays beneath
- The narrative ends on "Nobody's trying to win" with Brad holding the open kit
- This creates a direct handoff from booklet to physical unboxing

**Content Overview (Page by Page)**
- **Page 1 - Cover:**
  - Matte black, gold foil logo, white serif title
  - Typography only, no image
  - "From the Desk of Brad Sterling"
- **Page 2 - Arrival (Phase 1 opens):**
  - Day 1 onboarding as prison intake
  - Brad observing from his co-working space, doing "market research"
  - Two vignettes: corporate processing above, Brad mirroring below
- **Page 3 - Your Cell:**
  - The cubicle measured as 6x8
  - Death row is 6x9
  - "Enjoy the upgrade"
- **Page 4 - The Block:**
  - Office-as-prison vocabulary deepens
  - "Been here longer than the carpet smell. The carpet's union."
  - Barcode tag and union membership card as visual props
- **Page 5 - The Instincts:**
  - Reversal on page 4 - not the people, the survival behaviors
  - Defensive desk arrangements, territorial markers, hoarded supplies
  - Two-line gut punch: "Not the people. The instincts."
- **Pages 6-7 - Conference Room B (Phase 2 opens, spread):**
  - Brad plants a pen in a meeting room
  - By Thursday it has a name, a reputation, and three people afraid of it
  - Origin story for the Cubicle Shank - the office created it, Brad just watched
- **Pages 8-9 - The Loadout (spread):**
  - Morning routine reframed as tactical equipment loadout
  - Coffee = Nerve Agent, Headphones = Perimeter Defense, Laptop = Shield, Pen = Cubicle Shank
  - Format subversion: military/video game equipment diagram applied to office supplies
- **Pages 10-11 - The Ink (spread):**
  - Status symbols dismissed ("None of it proves anything")
  - Real marks: the lunch that didn't make it, being a flight risk
  - "You earned your ink. You just haven't worn it yet."
  - Primes the Lifer Ink tattoos without naming the product
- **Pages 12-13 - The Code (spread):**
  - Office speak decoded ("Sounds good" means no, "Let's circle back" means never)
  - "Per my last email" escalates to career threat
  - "I just put it in writing."
  - Primes all three card decks without naming them
- **Pages 14-15 - The Reveal (spread):**
  - Brad's full portrait with open briefcase, presenting the kit
  - "Everybody's trying to survive. That's the problem. Nobody's trying to win."
  - Tonal flip from dark comedy to aspirational hype
  - Dark background signals the finale
- **Page 16 - Back Cover:**
  - Matte black, matching front cover framing
  - Dense legal comedy fine print for close readers
  - Escalating absurdity: patents claimed but not filed, intern compensated in exposure
  - Contact emails: support, compliments, and "please hold" for complaints
