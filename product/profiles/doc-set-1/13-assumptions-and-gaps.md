# Artifact 13: Assumptions and Gaps

---

## Assumptions Made in This Profile

1. **The three-act narrative structure will survive page count changes**
   - The current Assessment (pages 2-5), Diagnosis (pages 6-13), Intervention (pages 14-15) allocation assumes exactly 16 pages
   - If the page count shifts for production reasons, the act boundaries and pacing will need to be rebalanced
   - Saddle-stitch binding requires page counts in multiples of four, which constrains adjustment options to 12 or 20

2. **Gold foil COGS on the cover are acceptable within the kit's unit economics**
   - Gold foil on matte black is specified as the cover treatment
   - This profile assumes the foil cost at volume is within the booklet's allocated COGS budget
   - If foil is cut, the cover must still signal premium - debossing or spot UV may be fallback options, but they have not been evaluated

3. **Editorial illustrations will match the style direction in the design system**
   - The design system specifies illustration style, but illustrations have not been generated
   - This profile assumes the final illustrations will achieve the intended tone (satirical but polished, character-consistent, editorially composed)
   - Illustration quality directly affects whether the booklet reads as premium or as amateur

4. **Recipients will engage with the booklet before unboxing kit items**
   - The booklet is placed on Tray 1 to physically gate access to items
   - This profile assumes most recipients will at least open the booklet and read pages 2-3 before reaching underneath
   - If recipients consistently set the booklet aside unopened, the narrative priming effect is lost

5. **The three-act structure enhances unboxing pacing rather than delaying it**
   - The narrative arc is designed to build anticipation, not frustrate it
   - This profile assumes recipients experience the booklet as entertainment that makes the items more exciting, not as an obstacle between them and the products
   - If user testing reveals impatience, the hook on pages 2-3 may need to be sharpened or the booklet may need a "read me first" prompt on the cover

6. **The indirect item references will trigger retroactive recognition**
   - Conference Room B, "earned your ink," and "put it in writing" are calibrated to be vague during reading and specific during unboxing
   - This profile assumes recipients will make these connections naturally
   - If the references are too oblique, the booklet and items will feel disconnected rather than mutually reinforcing

7. **Brad Sterling's comedy voice works across the target demographic range**
   - The corporate satire assumes familiarity with office culture, performance reviews, corporate jargon, and workplace dynamics
   - This profile assumes the voice lands for the 25-55 age range with office experience
   - Recipients without corporate work experience may find the satire less relatable, though the absurdity may still entertain on its own

---

## Known Gaps

1. **Illustration generation has not started**
   - The design system defines style direction, but no page illustrations have been created
   - Illustration quality is a major variable in whether the booklet achieves editorial-grade production or falls short
   - Image generation prompts for individual pages are incomplete

2. **Page-by-page image prompts are not finalized**
   - The copy document defines narrative content per page, but the corresponding visual direction per page has not been fully specified
   - Each format subversion (loadout, decoder, portrait) requires distinct illustration approaches that have not been tested

3. **No physical mockup has been produced or tested**
   - All evaluation to date has been digital
   - Paper stock feel, foil reflectivity, ink density on cream interior, and saddle-stitch spine behavior at 16 pages are untested
   - Fine print readability at small point sizes on the specified paper stock is unconfirmed

4. **Print partner has not been selected**
   - COGS estimates are based on general market rates, not quoted prices from a specific vendor
   - Gold foil capability, minimum order quantities, paper stock availability, and turnaround times are unconfirmed
   - Print partner selection will affect final production specifications and may require design adjustments

5. **The tonal flip on pages 14-15 has not been user-tested**
   - The shift from dark comedy to aspirational hype is the structural surprise of the three-act narrative
   - If this transition feels jarring rather than earned, the ending could undermine the preceding 12 pages
   - This is the highest-risk creative decision in the booklet and has no test data

6. **Booklet interaction data does not exist**
   - There is no data on how long recipients spend with the booklet, which pages get the most attention, or whether the booklet drives second reads
   - All engagement assumptions in this profile are based on structural analysis and comedy writing principles, not observed behavior

7. **Social shareability of individual pages is theoretical**
   - The loadout spread, decoder card, and fine print are positioned as shareable assets, but no content has been created or tested in social formats
   - Whether these pages photograph well, read clearly at phone-screen resolution, and generate engagement is unverified
