# Artifact 9: Buyer Questions Map

Note: These questions are specific to the Mug Shot item as it appears within the kit listing and unboxing experience.

**Questions the buyer asks when evaluating the listing**
- Is there really a camp mug in there?
- What does the mug look like? (looking for design shots)
- What does the "classified" item turn out to be?
- Is the mug a real size or one of those tiny novelty cups?

**Questions the recipient asks during unboxing**
- Wait, is there something behind this panel? (discovering the compartment cover)
- What does this say? (reading the compartment cover teaser text)
- Is that shredded paper? What is all this? (digging through classified documents)
- Oh my god, is that a MUG? (finding the mug in the compartment)
- What's inside the mug? (discovering the easter egg card)

**Questions the group asks while watching**
- What's behind there? (seeing the recipient discover the compartment)
- Read that thing! (pointing at the compartment cover)
- What is it?! (anticipation while recipient digs through documents)
- Wait, there's a MUG SHOT? (the name lands and the group reacts)
- Is there anything else? (checking if the kit has more surprises)

**Questions asked after unboxing**
- Where'd you get that mug? (visitors noticing the mug on desk or in kitchen)
- What's "Mug Shot"? (the name prompts the question, which prompts the story)
- Was that really hidden in the kit? (the reveal story is the retelling hook)

**Questions the buyer asks post-purchase**
- Did they find the hidden mug? (first thing gift-givers want to know)
- Did the [CLASSIFIED] thing land? (buyers who noticed the outer box treatment)
- Do they actually use the mug? (long-term satisfaction signal for gift-givers)
