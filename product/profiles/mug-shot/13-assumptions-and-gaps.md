# Artifact 13: Assumptions and Gaps

## Assumptions Made in This Profile

1. **The enamel camp mug will be the final material choice** - This profile assumes an enamel-coated steel camp mug. If COGS force a stainless steel mug without enamel, the visual aesthetic changes significantly and the "camp mug" positioning weakens.

2. **11oz capacity is achievable within budget** - Capacity directly affects perceived value. An 8oz mug reads as smaller and cheaper. This profile assumes 11oz is the target and that sourcing can deliver it within the kit's COGS target.

3. **The mugshot design will be single-face, not wrap** - Assuming the booking photo design appears on one face of the mug. A wrap print would increase cost but also increase visual impact. Design direction is not finalized.

4. **The hidden compartment reveal works as designed** - The entire Mug Shot strategy depends on the compartment cover being discoverable but not obvious. If recipients consistently miss the compartment, the finale doesn't happen. If it's too obvious, the surprise is lost.

5. **The shredded document packing material is feasible at scale** - Assuming custom-printed shredded paper (classified stamps, redacted text, incident reports) can be produced economically. If not, generic dark-colored shredded paper is a fallback but loses the content value.

6. **The easter egg card adds value without adding meaningful COGS** - Assuming a simple printed card can be placed inside the mug at minimal cost. If the card shifts during shipping, it needs to be visible upon first look inside the mug.

7. **Brad's [CLASSIFIED] treatment will appear on the outer box** - This profile assumes the outer Amazon box product grid lists item #7 as "[CLASSIFIED]" with a blacked-out image. If the outer box design changes, the pre-unboxing mystery element is lost.

## Known Gaps

1. **Mug sourcing not started** - No samples requested. Cost difference between enamel and stainless steel unknown. Print durability untested.

2. **Mugshot design not created** - The booking photo design direction is conceptual only. No art direction, no typography choices, no specific prisoner number or height line treatment decided.

3. **Compartment cover copy not written** - Teaser copy (top surface) and payoff copy (underside) are described conceptually but no actual writing exists.

4. **Easter egg card copy not finalized** - Working copy exists ("You looked inside. Smart. That instinct will keep you alive in here.") but this hasn't been vetted against the overall kit voice.

5. **No physical reveal testing done** - The hidden compartment reveal sequence has not been tested with real people. Timing, discoverability, and emotional impact are theoretical.

6. **Shredded document content not designed** - The classified stamps, redacted text, and incident report fragments on the shredded paper need actual content creation. This is technically copy work but affects production design.

7. **Mug dimensions vs. compartment fit not confirmed** - Working estimate of ~3.5" diameter x 3.5" tall. The compartment must accommodate the mug lying on its side with room for shredded document packing.

8. **COGS impact on kit margin unknown** - The mug is likely the highest-COGS single item ($4-6 enamel, $2-3 stainless). Its impact on the overall kit margin needs modeling against the total COGS budget.
