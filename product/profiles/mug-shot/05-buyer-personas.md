# Artifact 5: Buyer Personas

Note: The Mug Shot is not sold individually. These personas describe how each buyer type interacts with this specific item as part of the kit evaluation and unboxing experience.

## Persona 1 - "The Value Calculator"

**Who**
- 30-55, any gender
- Mentally prices every component in a bundle to see if the total exceeds the asking price
- Will scrutinize listing images to inventory what's included

**Relationship to the Mug Shot**
- The mug is the item that tips their mental math from "maybe" to "worth it"
- They'll look for the mug in listing images and estimate its standalone value ($8-15)
- Combined with the pen, the mug gives them two "real" items that anchor the kit price as fair
- If the mug looks cheap or small, the math doesn't work and they move on

**Key trigger**
- "Okay, a real camp mug AND a decent pen plus all this other stuff? That's actually a good deal"

**Biggest fear**
- The mug is tiny, flimsy, or obviously cheap in person despite looking decent in photos

---

## Persona 2 - "The Unboxing Completionist"

**Who**
- 22-40, any gender
- Will explore every corner of the kit, check every pocket, lift every tray
- Won't stop until they've found everything

**Relationship to the Mug Shot**
- This is the person the hidden compartment is designed for
- They'll find the compartment cover, read both sides, dig through the shredded documents, and discover the mug
- The easter egg card inside the mug ("You looked inside. Smart.") rewards their thoroughness
- The multi-step reveal is their favorite part of the entire kit

**Key trigger**
- "Wait. There's something behind here..." (discovering the hidden compartment)

**Biggest fear**
- The hidden compartment is obvious or the reveal doesn't feel intentional

---

## Persona 3 - "The Kitchen Curator"

**Who**
- 25-45, any gender
- Has opinions about their mug collection
- Curates their kitchen/desk items to reflect personality

**Relationship to the Mug Shot**
- This mug goes directly into their daily rotation
- The camp mug aesthetic (enamel, wire handle) fits a curated kitchen
- The mugshot design is a conversation piece that stands out against plain mugs
- They'll keep it visible because it says something about them

**Key trigger**
- "This is my new work mug" (immediate adoption into daily use)

**Biggest fear**
- The mug design is too busy, crude, or cheaply printed to use proudly

---

## Persona 4 - "The Punchline Repeater"

**Who**
- 25-50, any gender
- Will tell everyone about the funniest part of a gift
- Retells jokes and story moments to people who weren't there

**Relationship to the Mug Shot**
- "Mug Shot" is the item name they'll repeat to everyone
- The double meaning is the single most retellable joke in the kit
- "It's called a MUG SHOT - get it?" becomes their go-to description of the kit
- The [CLASSIFIED] hidden compartment reveal is the story they tell about the unboxing

**Key trigger**
- "Oh my god, MUG SHOT" (the name clicks and they say it aloud)

**Biggest fear**
- The design on the mug doesn't match the clever name - the physical object needs to live up to the punchline
