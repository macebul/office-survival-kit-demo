# Artifact 6: Jobs To Be Done

## Primary JTBD

**When** I discover the hidden compartment and find the Mug Shot camp mug
**I want to** feel that this kit saved its best for last
**So that** the entire unboxing experience feels like a complete, well-designed comedy show with a satisfying finale

## Supporting JTBD

**Job 2 - Validate the Kit's Price**
- When I've seen all the items and I'm forming my opinion of the kit, I want the mug to feel like a genuinely valuable item ($8-15 worth), so that the total perceived value clearly exceeds what was paid

**Job 3 - Give Me Something I'll Use Every Day**
- When the unboxing is over and the excitement settles, I want a camp mug that's good enough to become my daily coffee mug, so that the kit gives me lasting utility alongside the comedy

**Job 4 - Surprise Me After I Think I've Found Everything**
- When I think the unboxing is over after Tray 2, I want the hidden compartment to reveal one more item, so that the kit exceeds my expectations for how much content is inside

**Job 5 - Give Me the Easiest Joke to Retell**
- When I tell people about the kit, I want one item name that instantly communicates the humor, so that I can sell the concept in two words. "It has a camp mug called Mug Shot." Done.

**Job 6 - Close the Experience With a Physical Punchline**
- When I pull the mug out of shredded classified documents, I want the visual moment to feel like a culmination of the entire prison/office theme, so that the finale feels intentional, not random

## Underserved Jobs (How Competitor Gift Kit Mugs Fail)

- The "genuine quality" job - most gag gift mugs are thin ceramic with crude prints that chip and fade. Camp mug enamel feels premium and functional
- The "hidden reveal" job - no competitor hides their highest-value item behind a multi-step reveal sequence. Mugs are just sitting in the box, visible from the start
- The "punchline that explains itself" job - most funny mugs have text that's either too long, too crude, or too obscure. "Mug Shot" is a two-word punchline that needs no setup
- The "daily use without embarrassment" job - most gag gift mugs are too crude or too obviously novelty for professional settings. A camp mug with a booking photo design is conversation-starting without being offensive
