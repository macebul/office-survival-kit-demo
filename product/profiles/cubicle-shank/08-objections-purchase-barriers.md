# Artifact 8: Objections and Purchase Barriers

Note: Most purchase objections apply to the kit as a whole. These objections are specific to how the Cubicle Shank item influences or creates hesitation.

**Objection 1 - "Is that pen actually decent or is it a cheap toy?"**
- Root cause: Years of gag gifts with novelty pens that break after one use
- Evidence needed: Close-up images showing metal construction, professional finish, and real writing capability
- Mitigation: Listing images should show the pen at a quality level that reads as "$10-15 tactical pen," not as "dollar store filler." The material and weight should be visible

**Objection 2 - "A tactical pen in a gag gift feels overdone"**
- Root cause: Tactical pens are a cliche in the men's gift category on Amazon
- Evidence needed: Proof that this pen is part of a comedy experience, not just another tactical pen listing
- Mitigation: The packaging copy and the "Cubicle Shank" name immediately differentiate this from serious tactical pen products. The comedy context transforms a commodity item into a unique experience

**Objection 3 - "Will the barrel engraving wear off?"**
- Root cause: Printed text on pens often rubs off with use, feeling cheap
- Evidence needed: Confirmation of engraving vs. printing method durability
- Mitigation: Prefer laser engraving over pad printing. If printing is necessary, select a method tested for durability under daily handling

**Objection 4 - "The box is funny but is it too dense to read?"**
- Root cause: Small text on narrow surfaces might not be scannable in a social setting
- Evidence needed: Physical mockup testing at actual size to confirm readability at arm's length
- Mitigation: Typographic hierarchy must prioritize the single funniest line at a size readable from 3-4 feet. Secondary content can be smaller and reward closer examination

**Objection 5 - "Is this the right item to lead with in the unboxing?"**
- Root cause: If the pen doesn't grab attention in the tray, it might be picked up second or third
- Evidence needed: The box should visually distinguish itself from Lifer Ink and Turf Tags boxes in the same tray
- Mitigation: The matte black with white/red type already contrasts against other boxes. The longer matchbox format structurally differentiates it. Center placement in the tray reinforces hero status
