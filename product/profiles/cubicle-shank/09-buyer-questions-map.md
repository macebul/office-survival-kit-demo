# Artifact 9: Buyer Questions Map

Note: These questions are specific to the Cubicle Shank item as it appears within the kit listing and unboxing experience.

**Questions the buyer asks when evaluating the listing**
- Is that pen real or a toy?
- What does the box say on it?
- Is the pen actually usable or just for laughs?
- Does it look like something a grown adult would keep on their desk?

**Questions the recipient asks during unboxing**
- What does this box say? (reaches for it)
- Wait, is this a real pen? (picks it up, feels the weight)
- What does it say on the barrel? (reads "FOR OFFICE USE ONLY")
- Can I try it? (writes something to confirm it works)

**Questions the group asks while watching**
- What does that box say? (leaning in or asking the opener to read it)
- Read the instructions on the side! (pointing at the Field Deployment steps)
- Does it actually write? (curiosity about functional quality)

**Questions asked after unboxing**
- Where'd you get that pen? (desk visitors noticing the engraving)
- What's the box from? (noticing the box as a desk artifact)
- Can I get one of these kits? (the pen as a gateway to kit awareness)

**Questions the buyer asks post-purchase**
- Did they like the pen? (first question gift-givers ask recipients)
- Does the company make just the pen separately? (interest in standalone product)
