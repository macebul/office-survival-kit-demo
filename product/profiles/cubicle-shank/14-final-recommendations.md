# Artifact 14: Final Recommendations

**Recommendation 1 - Resolve the Naming Hierarchy Before Finalizing the Box**
- The single biggest blocker for the Cubicle Shank box is whether "Cubicle Shank" or "Executive Tactical Stylus™" gets hero treatment on the top face
- This decision cascades to every other item box in the kit
- Run a dedicated session to establish the naming system kit-wide, then update all box layouts simultaneously

**Recommendation 2 - Sample 2-3 Pen Styles Before Committing**
- The pen quality is the quality anchor for the entire kit. Do not source the cheapest option
- Request samples of at least 2-3 tactical pen styles in the $2-4 COGS range
- Evaluate each for weight, writing quality, finish durability, and "first impression" feel
- The pen should pass the "hand it to someone without context and they'll say it's a nice pen" test

**Recommendation 3 - Test the Box at Actual Print Size**
- Design files look great on screen but the box is physically narrow (1.75" wide, 1" edges)
- Print a 1:1 mockup and test readability at arm's length and at 3-4 feet
- Confirm the "Field Deployment" steps on the long edge are readable at 1" height
- If text is too small, prioritize fewer words at larger size over cramming everything in

**Recommendation 4 - Prioritize Engraving Over Printing for the Barrel**
- "FOR OFFICE USE ONLY" needs to survive daily handling for months
- Laser engraving is more durable and feels more premium than pad printing
- Get cost quotes for both and strongly prefer engraving unless the cost delta is unreasonable

**Recommendation 5 - Design the Short Ends as Visible Tray Elements**
- When the box sits in its tray recess, the open sleeve ends (1.75" x 1.0") face upward
- These are the surfaces the recipient sees before picking up the box
- Even minimal treatment (catalog numbers, classification marks, Elevate Co. branding) adds institutional authenticity

**Recommendation 6 - Confirm the Comedy Hierarchy on the Top Face**
- Identify which single line is the "read aloud" line for the group
- That line needs to be scannable at arm's length within 2-3 seconds
- Every other element on the top face serves this one line by creating context around it
- Test with 2-3 people who haven't seen the box: "What's the first thing you read?"

**Recommendation 7 - Consider the Pen as a Standalone SKU Later**
- The Cubicle Shank has the strongest "I'd buy this by itself" signal of any kit item
- After the kit launches and establishes the brand, a standalone Cubicle Shank listing could extend the product line
- This is a future consideration, not a current priority - do not let it complicate the kit launch
