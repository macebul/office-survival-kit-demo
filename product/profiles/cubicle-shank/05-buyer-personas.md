# Artifact 5: Buyer Personas

Note: The Cubicle Shank is not sold individually. These personas describe how each buyer type interacts with this specific item as part of the kit evaluation and unboxing experience.

## Persona 1 - "The Quality Skeptic"

**Who**
- 30-50, any gender
- Has been burned by cheap Amazon gag gifts before
- Reads reviews carefully, zooms into product images

**Relationship to the Cubicle Shank**
- This is the item they scrutinize hardest in the listing photos
- If the pen looks cheap in images, they're out
- If the pen looks premium, they start believing the rest of the kit is worth the price

**Key trigger**
- "Wait, that pen actually looks like a real tactical pen"

**Biggest fear**
- Opening the box at the party and realizing the pen is a flimsy piece of plastic

---

## Persona 2 - "The Read-It-Aloud Performer"

**Who**
- 25-40, any gender
- The person at the party who makes everything funnier by narrating it
- Will pick up the pen box, pause, and dramatically read every surface

**Relationship to the Cubicle Shank**
- The box is their prop. The pen is secondary to the performance
- They'll read "FIELD DEPLOYMENT" instructions to the group like an announcement
- They'll slide the sleeve off slowly for dramatic effect

**Key trigger**
- "Hold on, hold on - listen to this" (reads box copy aloud)

**Biggest fear**
- There's nothing funny enough on the packaging to perform with

---

## Persona 3 - "The Desk Display Collector"

**Who**
- 25-45, any gender
- Keeps interesting objects on their desk (Funko pops, desk toys, novelty items)
- Values aesthetic objects with personality

**Relationship to the Cubicle Shank**
- Both the pen and the box become desk artifacts
- The "FOR OFFICE USE ONLY" engraving gets noticed by visitors
- The box stays on display even after the pen is in the pen cup

**Key trigger**
- "This is going on my desk immediately"

**Biggest fear**
- The pen looks cool in photos but cheap in person

---

## Persona 4 - "The Practical Justifier"

**Who**
- 30-55, any gender
- Needs to rationalize fun purchases with utility
- The person who says "well at least it's useful" about gifts

**Relationship to the Cubicle Shank**
- This item is their permission slip for enjoying the rest of the kit
- "It's not just a gag gift - it comes with a really nice pen"
- They'll use the pen daily and it becomes their go-to "where'd you get that?" conversation starter

**Key trigger**
- "Okay but honestly, this pen is actually really solid"

**Biggest fear**
- The pen runs out of ink in a week and there's no way to replace it
