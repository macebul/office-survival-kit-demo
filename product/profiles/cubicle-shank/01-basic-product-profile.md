# Artifact 1: Basic Product Profile

**Product Name**
- Cubicle Shank

**Brad Sterling's Corporate Rebrand**
- Executive Tactical Stylus™

**Parent Product**
- Office Survival Kit (Item 1 of 7 + 1 empty recess)

**Brand**
- Witty Yeti (Amazon brand)
- Elevate Co. (in-universe fictional company, appears in product copy)

**Product Type**
- Tactical-style ballpoint pen with custom printed item box

**Role in Kit**
- Hero item
- First item discovered in Tray 1 (center recess)
- Sets the tone for the entire unboxing experience
- Establishes the quality bar that tells the recipient "this isn't a cheap gag gift"

**Physical Specs**
- Style: Tactical/survival pen aesthetic (angular, aggressive design)
- Material: Aluminum or metal body (matte black preferred)
- Length: ~5.5" x 0.6" (standard pen dimensions)
- Ink: Black, replaceable cartridge preferred
- Weight: Should feel substantial, not cheap
- Features: Glass breaker tip optional but on-brand; clip for pocket carry

**Item Box**
- Box type: Tray-and-sleeve (matchbox style)
- Outer sliding sleeve + inner tray with die-cut pen recess
- Box dimensions: ~6.5" x 1.75" x 1" (working estimate)
- Material: Printed paperboard (14-18pt)
- Print: Full color, all sides
- Design: Brad Sterling's Elevate Co. retail branding
- Interior: Die-cut recess shaped for pen, visible copy area around/below pen

**Customization**
- Engraved or printed text on barrel
- Location: Along the barrel, visible when held
- Text: "FOR OFFICE USE ONLY"

**Approved Copy**
- Subheader: "It writes. Among other things."
- Packaging panel copy: "Some call it a pen. Survivors call it a Cubicle Shank. Ideal for intimidating your way to the last donut in the break room. Sure, it writes - but its real value is the reminder: you're not just taking notes. You're taking names."
- Interior reveal: "THE PEN IS MIGHTIER THAN THE SWORD. QUIETER, TOO."

**Discovery Context**
- Tray 1, narrow front recess (centered)
- First tray revealed after lifting Doc Set 1
- Flanked by Lifer Ink (left) and Turf Tags (right)
- Recipient picks it up, reads the box, then slides the sleeve open
