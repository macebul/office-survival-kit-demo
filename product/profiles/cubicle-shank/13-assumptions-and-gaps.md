# Artifact 13: Assumptions and Gaps

## Assumptions Made in This Profile

1. **The pen quality will match the positioning** - This profile assumes a metal-body pen with real weight and a smooth writing experience. If COGS force a plastic body or cheap mechanism, the entire "quality anchor" strategy collapses and every downstream item suffers.

2. **The engraving will be durable** - "FOR OFFICE USE ONLY" is a long-term comedy element that only works if it stays visible after months of daily use. Assuming laser engraving or equivalent durability.

3. **The matchbox mechanism will feel premium** - The tray-and-sleeve construction needs to slide smoothly and feel intentional. If the paperboard is too thin (below 18pt), the box will feel flimsy and undercut the reveal moment.

4. **The box dimensions work at 6.5" x 1.75" x 1"** - These are working estimates. The tray recess in the briefcase needs to accommodate the actual final dimensions, and the copy layouts are designed for these proportions.

5. **Center placement in Tray 1 drives first-pick behavior** - Assuming the recipient naturally reaches for the center item first. If they reach for a side item (Lifer Ink or Turf Tags), the Cubicle Shank loses its "opening act" advantage.

6. **The dual naming system will be resolved kit-wide** - This profile assumes "Cubicle Shank" gets hero treatment and "Executive Tactical Stylus™" plays the institutional header role. If the naming hierarchy inverts, the box layout and comedy delivery change significantly.

## Known Gaps

1. **Final pen sourcing not complete** - No samples selected. Cost difference between aluminum and steel unknown. Multiple tactical pen styles need sampling.

2. **Kit-wide naming hierarchy not decided** - The relationship between prison terms and Brad's corporate rebrands is not locked across all items. This blocks the top face layout of the box.

3. **Item box copy not finalized** - Brad's box-specific copy (distinct from the packaging panel copy in the copy document) needs a dedicated writing session.

4. **Box dimensions unconfirmed** - 6.5" x 1.75" x 1" is a working estimate pending sourcing partner confirmation.

5. **Short end treatments not designed** - The open sleeve ends (1.75" x 1.0") are visible when the box sits in the tray recess. No design exists for these surfaces yet.

6. **No physical mockup tested** - Typography readability, matchbox mechanism feel, and interior reveal impact have not been tested at actual print size with real people.

7. **Engraving vs. printing decision pending** - Barrel text method affects durability, cost, and appearance. Samples needed.
