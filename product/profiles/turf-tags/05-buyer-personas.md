# Artifact 5: Buyer Personas

Note: Turf Tags are not sold individually. These personas describe how each buyer type interacts with this specific item as part of the kit evaluation and unboxing experience.

## Persona 1 - "The Territory Defender"

**Who**
- 25-45, any gender
- Fiercely protective of their desk space, lunch, and office supplies
- Has strong opinions about shared spaces and boundary violations

**Relationship to Turf Tags**
- This item speaks directly to their lived experience
- They'll deploy stickers within 24 hours of unboxing, starting with their lunch container
- They see the stickers as both funny and genuinely useful
- Each sticker is a passive-aggressive message they've always wanted to send

**Key trigger**
- "Finally. Someone understands." (reads "SHARING IS CARING OVER.")

**Biggest fear**
- The sticker designs are too generic or cute - they need to actually say something

---

## Persona 2 - "The Office Vandal"

**Who**
- 22-35, any gender
- Enjoys harmless pranks, desk decorations, and subtle chaos
- Will put a sticker on someone else's stuff just to see their reaction

**Relationship to Turf Tags**
- These stickers are prank ammunition
- They'll deploy them on coworkers' desks, chairs, and monitors (not just their own)
- The territorial claim on someone else's property is funnier than on their own
- They're the reason stickers travel the furthest from the original recipient

**Key trigger**
- "Which one do I put on my boss's chair?"

**Biggest fear**
- The stickers aren't funny enough to justify the bit

---

## Persona 3 - "The Dismisser"

**Who**
- 30-50, any gender
- Sees "stickers" listed in the kit and assumes it's the weakest item
- Has low expectations before picking up the yellow box

**Relationship to Turf Tags**
- This is the persona the box packaging is designed to convert
- The yellow color and dog illustration create curiosity before the text is read
- The instruction panel and fine print escalation build genuine surprise
- If the box earns a laugh, the stickers get a chance. If the box is boring, the stickers stay in it

**Key trigger**
- "Okay, the box is actually really funny" (converts from dismissal to engagement)

**Biggest fear**
- Their initial assumption was right and the stickers are just filler

---

## Persona 4 - "The Collector/Saver"

**Who**
- 25-40, any gender
- Doesn't want to "use up" things - hoards special stickers, nice pens, limited edition items
- Will admire the stickers but hesitate to deploy them

**Relationship to Turf Tags**
- With only 6-12 stickers, scarcity triggers their preservation instinct
- They'll pick a favorite and protect it rather than deploy it
- The tuck flap instruction ("START WITH THE FRIDGE.") tries to override this impulse with a specific action prompt
- They may keep the yellow box on display because it's the most visually striking object from the kit

**Key trigger**
- "These are too good to waste" (appreciates the designs but hesitates to deploy)

**Biggest fear**
- Regretting where they deployed a sticker because they can't get more
