# Artifact 14: Final Recommendations

**Recommendation 1 - Lock the Sticker Count and Update the Box Copy**
- The box says "CONTAINS: 12" but the copy document suggests 6-8
- This is the single most critical consistency issue for this item
- Decide on the final count based on COGS, strip size, and perceived value
- 12 stickers strengthens value perception but requires more designs. 8 stickers with stronger individual designs may be better than 12 with filler

**Recommendation 2 - Finalize Sticker Designs Through a Deployment Test**
- Don't just ask "which designs are funniest" - ask "which would you actually stick somewhere?"
- Give 3-5 people the 10 concepts (printed at actual size) and ask them to identify deployment targets
- Track which designs get assigned to specific surfaces vs. which just get "yeah, that's funny"
- A sticker that's funny but has no natural deployment surface is not a strong sticker for this product

**Recommendation 3 - Confirm Vinyl vs. Paper With the Production Partner**
- Get COGS quotes for both materials at the expected kit volume
- If vinyl is within budget, use vinyl - it supports the "permanent claim" messaging and survives on surfaces that get wiped
- If paper is necessary for COGS, adjust the copy accordingly - don't promise durability you can't deliver

**Recommendation 4 - Audit the Front Face Fine Print Zone**
- Every line below the red divider needs to earn its space
- Read each line in isolation and ask: "Does this get a reaction, or is it just atmosphere?"
- Cut lines that are institutional atmosphere without comedy payoff - they add density without earning laughs
- Resolve the six-stars vs. "9/5 STARS" redundancy before print

**Recommendation 5 - Test the Instruction Panel Readability at Actual Size**
- The IKEA-style icons (PEEL / STICK / HOLD COURT / ACCEPT PARADE / EXIT) are the box's best read-aloud element
- They sit on the top edge (~0.5" height), which is a very narrow surface
- Print at 1:1 and confirm the icons and labels are legible at arm's length
- If they're too small, consider moving the instruction panel to a larger surface or increasing icon size at the expense of some text

**Recommendation 6 - Place the Yellow Box Prominently in Listing Photos**
- The yellow color and dog illustration are the most visually striking elements in the kit
- In the hero shot and content-spread listing images, the yellow box should be visible and front-facing
- The color contrast against the matte black briefcase creates a "pop" that draws the eye in thumbnails
- The dog illustration is legible enough to communicate the concept even at search-result thumbnail size

**Recommendation 7 - Pair Turf Tags and Lifer Ink Side by Side at Actual Size**
- Both boxes share the same tray recess
- Print both boxes at actual size and place them in a mockup tray to verify visual distinction
- The yellow vs. cream/gold should read as clearly different items, not color variants of the same thing
- Verify that each box has its own identity at tray-viewing distance (18-24 inches)
