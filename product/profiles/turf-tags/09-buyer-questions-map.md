# Artifact 9: Buyer Questions Map

Note: These questions are specific to the Turf Tags item as it appears within the kit listing and unboxing experience.

**Questions the Buyer Asks When Evaluating the Listing**
- What's the yellow thing? (immediately drawn to the color contrast in product photos)
- Are those stickers? What do they say?
- Is that a dog peeing on a chair? (the illustration triggers curiosity)
- Would my coworker actually put these somewhere?

**Questions the Recipient Asks During Unboxing**
- What's this yellow box? (picks it up first or second because the color demands attention)
- Oh my god, is that a dog peeing on a chair? (the illustration lands)
- Wait, read the steps on the side! (discovers the IKEA instruction panel)
- "HOLD COURT"? "ACCEPT PARADE"? What is this? (reads aloud, group laughs at escalation)
- "START WITH THE FRIDGE." - okay, I know what I'm doing with these (opens box with a plan)

**Questions the Group Asks While Watching**
- What does that yellow box say? (the color draws attention from across the room)
- Read the instructions! (pointing at the top edge)
- Which sticker are you putting where? (already planning deployment)
- Can I have one? (the territorial claim creates ironic desire - now THEY want to mark territory too)

**Questions Asked After Deployment**
- Whose sticker is that? (coworker noticing a deployed sticker on shared equipment)
- Where did you get those? (the target question for social reach)
- Are there more? (interest in additional stickers or the full kit)
- Can I get a set? (the deployed sticker becomes a gateway to kit awareness)

**Questions the Buyer Asks Post-Purchase**
- Did they put the stickers anywhere? (hoping for deployment)
- Which one was their favorite? (curiosity about design preferences)
- Did anyone at the office react? (hoping the social moment happened)
