# Artifact 13: Assumptions and Gaps

## Assumptions Made in This Profile

1. **The yellow box will create positive visual contrast, not dissonance** - This profile assumes the bold yellow reads as "caution tape / territorial" rather than "doesn't match the kit." If the yellow feels like a random color choice rather than intentional territorial vocabulary, the box loses its conceptual anchor.

2. **Recipients will actually deploy stickers** - The deployment framing and "START WITH THE FRIDGE." instruction are designed to activate behavior, but deployment rates for included stickers in gift kits are historically very low. This hasn't been tested.

3. **6-12 stickers is the right count** - The box currently says "CONTAINS: 12" but the copy document suggests 6-8. More stickers add value but may require a larger strip or multiple strips. Fewer stickers strengthen the scarcity mechanic but may feel thin.

4. **The dog illustration will print well at box size** - The black silhouette should reproduce cleanly, but the illustration quality needs to be verified at actual print dimensions on actual paperboard stock.

5. **Vinyl stickers are cost-viable at kit COGS** - Vinyl is preferred for durability and the "permanent claim" messaging, but paper stickers may be necessary if vinyl pushes COGS beyond budget. Paper stickers undercut the "permanent territory" positioning.

6. **The "Territorial Assessment Report" back face works as comedy** - Brad's institutional form format (checkboxes, expected outcomes, Chief Territorial Officer title) is strong in concept but hasn't been tested with real people. Some recipients may skip the back face entirely.

7. **The naming hierarchy is settled** - This profile assumes "Turf Tags" gets hero treatment and "Workspace Personalization Labels&trade;" plays the Brad rebrand role. If the naming hierarchy inverts kit-wide, the front face layout changes.

## Known Gaps

1. **Sticker count not finalized** - Box says 12, copy document says 6-8. Must resolve before box copy is locked. The count affects perceived value, strip size, and COGS.

2. **Final sticker designs not selected** - 10 working concepts exist (5 Tier 1, 3 Tier 2, 2 Tier 3) plus 4 potential additions. Final selection requires stakeholder feedback on which designs are funniest and most deployable.

3. **Sticker material not confirmed** - Vinyl vs. paper affects durability, cost, finish, and the "permanent claim" messaging. Need quotes from production partner.

4. **Front face fine print density unaudited** - The zone below the red divider line is text-heavy. Some lines may not earn their space. Need a line-by-line audit to cut anything that doesn't contribute to comedy or institutional atmosphere.

5. **Left side text may not be legible at production scale** - "NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR DISPUTES, CONFRONTATIONS, OR AT ANYONE BIGGER THAN YOU." is a long line for a narrow surface (~0.5" height). Needs readability testing at actual size.

6. **Six stars on front face vs. "9/5 STARS" on right side** - Potential redundancy between two star-related elements. Need to clarify the relationship or cut one.

7. **Visual differentiation from Lifer Ink tested only in concept** - Both boxes share the same tray recess and similar dimensions. The yellow vs. cream/gold distinction needs to be verified at actual tray-viewing distance.

8. **No physical mockup tested** - Dog illustration impact, instruction panel readability, and overall box presence in the tray have not been validated with real people at real size.
