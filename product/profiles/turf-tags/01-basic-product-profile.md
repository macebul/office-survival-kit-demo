# Artifact 1: Basic Product Profile

**Product Name**
- Turf Tags

**Brad Sterling's Corporate Rebrand**
- Workspace Personalization Labels&trade;

**Parent Product**
- Office Survival Kit (Item 3 of 7 + 1 empty recess)

**Brand**
- Witty Yeti (Amazon brand)
- Elevate Co. (in-universe fictional company, appears in product copy)

**Division**
- Territorial Compliance Division

**Product Type**
- Die-cut adhesive stickers for workspace territory-marking, with custom printed tuck-end item box

**Role in Kit**
- The most physically deployable item in the kit - stickers leave the briefcase and enter the workplace
- Transforms "office supplies" into territory-marking tools
- Highest word-of-mouth potential - each deployed sticker is a visible conversation starter at someone's desk, fridge, or equipment
- Overcomes the "stickers are filler" perception through premium packaging and territorial framing

**Physical Specs - Sticker Strip**
- Strip size: ~4.5" x 1.25"
- Individual count: 6-12 stickers (final count pending - box currently reads "CONTAINS: 12")
- Individual sizes: Mix of small (~0.75") and medium (~1.5"-2")
- Material: Vinyl preferred (durable, water-resistant); paper acceptable if significant cost savings
- Finish: Matte or glossy (open to recommendation)
- Cut: Die-cut to shape preferred; kiss-cut acceptable

**Item Box**
- Box type: Tuck-end (flat format)
- Dimensions: ~5" x 3.5" x 0.5" (working estimate)
- Material: Printed paperboard (14-18pt)
- Print: Full color, all 6 exterior sides
- Color: Bold yellow (caution tape / warning sign / construction zone aesthetic)
- Design: Brad Sterling's Elevate Co. retail branding with "Territorial Compliance Division" header
- Hero illustration: Black silhouette of a dog lifting its leg on an office chair
- Interior tuck flap: "START WITH THE FRIDGE."

**Approved Copy**
- Subheader: "MARK YOUR TERRITORY."
- Subtitle: "Because Asking Nicely Didn't Work."
- Packaging panel copy: "Every desk. Every mug. Every stapler that 'wandered off.' Claim it. Tag it. Sleep soundly. These aren't decorations. They're boundaries."
- Bottom face thesis: "SHARING IS ~~CARING~~ OVER."

**Key Box Copy**
- Front fine print: "This product has been used as evidence in 3 workplace disputes and 1 divorce."
- Instruction panel (top edge): PEEL / STICK / HOLD COURT / ACCEPT PARADE / EXIT (IKEA-style icons)
- Right side: "9/5 STARS - BRAD STERLING" (Brad rates himself above the scale)
- Left side: "NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR DISPUTES, CONFRONTATIONS, OR AT ANYONE BIGGER THAN YOU."
- Back face: "TERRITORIAL ASSESSMENT REPORT / Form WPL-7" with Brad's photo and quote

**Discovery Context**
- Tray 1, wide back recess (right side, alongside Lifer Ink)
- Same tray as Cubicle Shank (center) and Lifer Ink (left)
- Yellow box creates immediate visual contrast against other items in the tray
- Dog silhouette visible from above communicates the concept before the box is picked up
