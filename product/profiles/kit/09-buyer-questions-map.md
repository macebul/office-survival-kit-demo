# Artifact 9: Buyer Questions Map

**Questions they ask before clicking the listing**
- Is this actually funny or just another generic gag gift?
- What's included for that price?
- Is this appropriate for a work setting?
- Will it arrive by [date]?

**Questions they ask on the listing page**
- What exactly comes in the kit?
- What do the items look like up close?
- Is the briefcase real quality or flimsy?
- What do the reviews say about the humor landing?
- Is this good for white elephant / Secret Santa / birthdays?

**Questions they ask before adding to cart**
- Is $25-30 worth it compared to other options I'm seeing?
- Will the person I'm buying for actually appreciate this?
- Is there anything inappropriate in here I should worry about?
- Can I see what the unboxing experience actually looks like?

**Questions they ask after purchase (pre-arrival)**
- When will it arrive?
- Does it come in gift-ready packaging or do I need to wrap it?

**Questions they ask after giving the gift**
- Where can I buy more for other people?
- Does this company make other products like this?
