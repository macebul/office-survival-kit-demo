# Artifact 5: Buyer Personas

## Persona 1 - "The White Elephant Winner"

**Who**
- 28-40, any gender
- Office worker buying for a holiday party exchange
- Budget-conscious but willing to spend more to "win" the exchange

**Behavior**
- Searches Amazon in November/December
- Reads reviews obsessively, looking for confirmation it's actually funny
- Compares 3-5 options before committing
- Wants the gift everyone fights over in the swap

**Key trigger**
- "This is the one that'll have everyone dying"

**Biggest fear**
- Buying something that gets a polite chuckle and then sits unclaimed

---

## Persona 2 - "The Thoughtful Joker"

**Who**
- 30-50, any gender
- Buying for a specific coworker's birthday, farewell, or promotion
- Knows the recipient well enough to know they'd appreciate office humor

**Behavior**
- Searches with intent ("funny gift for coworker who hates meetings")
- Cares about quality because it's a named gift, not anonymous
- Wants it to feel personal even though it's a mass product
- May add a handwritten note to personalize

**Key trigger**
- "This is exactly [person's name]"

**Biggest fear**
- It arriving and looking cheap or low-effort

---

## Persona 3 - "The Office Culture Builder"

**Who**
- 35-55, often a manager or team lead
- Buying for team morale, onboarding humor, or a team event
- May buy multiples

**Behavior**
- Less price-sensitive per unit
- Values appropriateness - humor needs to be office-safe
- Wants something that brings the team together
- May expense it

**Key trigger**
- "This would be hilarious for our team offsite"

**Biggest fear**
- HR getting involved because the humor crosses a line

---

## Persona 4 - "The Content-Aware Gifter"

**Who**
- 25-40, social media active
- Has seen an unboxing video or social post about the product
- Already sold on the concept, looking for the listing

**Behavior**
- Arrives with high intent from an external referral
- Skims reviews to confirm what they already believe
- Fast conversion, low comparison shopping
- Likely to film their own unboxing or the recipient's reaction

**Key trigger**
- "I saw this on TikTok and I need it for [person]"

**Biggest fear**
- That it won't be as good as the video made it look
