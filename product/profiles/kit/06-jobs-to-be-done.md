# Artifact 6: Jobs To Be Done

## Primary JTBD

**When** I need to buy a gift for someone who works in an office
**I want to** find something that's genuinely funny and feels like a real gift
**So that** the moment of giving is entertaining for everyone and the recipient actually keeps it

## Supporting JTBD

**Job 2 - Win the Exchange**
- When I'm going to a white elephant or Secret Santa party, I want to bring the gift everyone fights over, so that I get social credit for having the best find

**Job 3 - Express a Shared Experience**
- When I want to acknowledge that office life is absurd, I want to give something that validates that feeling through humor, so that the recipient feels seen and we bond over the shared joke

**Job 4 - Look Like a Thoughtful Gift-Giver**
- When I'm giving a gift to a coworker, I want it to look like I put real thought and money into it, so that I don't come across as someone who grabbed something last-minute

**Job 5 - Create a Group Moment**
- When I'm at a party or team event, I want the gift opening to be a shared experience, so that it entertains the room rather than just the recipient

**Job 6 - Generate Content**
- When someone opens this gift, I want the unboxing to be visually interesting and progressively surprising, so that it's worth filming and sharing

## Underserved Jobs (Currently Poorly Served by Competitors)

- The "sustained entertainment" job - most gag gifts deliver one punchline and they're done
- The "quality signal" job - most gag gifts look and feel disposable
- The "discovery" job - most gag gifts have no layers to uncover
- The "group experience" job - most gifts are opened, laughed at briefly, and set aside
