# Artifact 7: Prioritized Benefits

Ranked by likely impact on purchase decisions.

**Tier 1 - Must Communicate (drives the click and the conversion)**
1. It's genuinely funny with layers of humor, not a one-liner product
2. It looks and feels like a premium gift, not a cheap joke
3. It contains multiple items, creating a "wow, there's more" unboxing effect
4. It works perfectly for white elephant, birthdays, and office gifting

**Tier 2 - Should Communicate (builds confidence and reduces hesitation)**
5. The reveal sequence builds from item to item with a surprise ending
6. Individual items are actually usable (pen writes, mug is real, cards are playable)
7. The humor is office-universal, not niche or exclusionary
8. It's designed for group entertainment, not just the recipient

**Tier 3 - Nice to Communicate (differentiates for engaged shoppers)**
9. A fictional character ties the whole experience together with a consistent voice
10. Includes two produced booklets with original content
11. Interactive elements (tattoos, stickers, cards) extend life beyond unboxing
12. The empty recess is a meta-joke that shows commitment to the comedy
