# Artifact 13: Assumptions and Gaps

## Assumptions Made in This Profile

1. **4-6 tattoos per sheet is the right count** - This profile assumes the final sheet has 4-6 individual designs. More designs add value but may reduce individual tattoo size below readable threshold. Fewer designs may feel thin for the box size.

2. **Recipients will actually apply the tattoos** - The achievement card mechanic is designed to overcome the adoption barrier, but this hasn't been tested with real people. If application rates are very low, the item's social value strategy fails.

3. **The designs will be funny on skin** - What looks funny as a concept or on paper may not translate to temporary tattoo print quality. Clean lines and simple, graphic designs are assumed to reproduce well at small sizes.

4. **The achievement card mechanic will drive engagement** - The "earn your ink" framing assumes recipients respond to gamification. Some recipients may ignore the card entirely and just pick whichever tattoo they like.

5. **The cream/gold packaging will read as premium, not cheap** - If the gold accents look like foil stamping, it's premium. If they look like printed yellow, it undercuts the corporate award aesthetic entirely.

6. **The "Distinguished Honorees" back face concept is executable** - Corporate headshots of people wearing visible tattoos is the strongest comedy beat on the box, but the AI-generated portrait approach carries production risk. Alternative visual approaches may be needed.

7. **Box dimensions work at 5" x 3.5" x 0.5"** - The tattoo sheet, achievement card, and any backing material need to fit inside this box without feeling cramped. Dimensions are working estimates pending sourcing confirmation.

## Known Gaps

1. **Tattoo designs not finalized** - 21 working concepts exist but the final 4-6 (or 8-12 if expanding) have not been selected. Requires stakeholder feedback session to identify the funniest designs.

2. **Achievement card copy not written** - The concept is approved but no commendation requirements have been drafted. This is the mechanism that makes the product work and needs dedicated writing.

3. **Front face box copy needs revision** - Current concept buries the comedy under Brad's branding hierarchy. The front face must land a joke immediately, not just in fine print.

4. **Back face "Distinguished Honorees" execution undecided** - AI-generated corporate headshots with visible tattoos is the concept, but production method (AI photos vs. illustrated portraits vs. stylized photography) is unresolved.

5. **Tattoo print quality unverified** - No sample tattoos have been printed. Line thickness, color accuracy, and adhesion quality at this size are unknown. Need samples from production partner.

6. **Application behavior untested** - No real-world testing of whether people will actually apply the tattoos at work. The achievement card mechanic is theoretical.

7. **Visual differentiation from Turf Tags not finalized** - Both boxes share the same tray recess, same approximate dimensions, and similar flat format. Must feel distinct despite physical similarities.

8. **Naming hierarchy unresolved (same as all items)** - "Lifer Ink" vs. "Motivational Body Art Collection&trade;" hero treatment depends on the kit-wide naming decision.
