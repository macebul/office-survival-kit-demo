# Artifact 8: Objections and Purchase Barriers

Note: Most purchase objections apply to the kit as a whole. These objections are specific to how the Lifer Ink item influences or creates hesitation.

**Objection 1 - "Temporary tattoos? Aren't those for kids?"**
- Root cause: The category is strongly associated with children's party favors and vending machines
- Evidence needed: Premium packaging, adult humor on the designs, and achievement card mechanic that reframes the product for adults
- Mitigation: The cream/gold corporate award certificate packaging immediately signals "this is not a kids' product." The comedy content on the designs (office burnout, meeting survival) is adult-specific. The prison tattoo parallel adds edge that further distances from the juvenile association

**Objection 2 - "Nobody's actually going to put these on"**
- Root cause: Skepticism that adult recipients will apply temporary tattoos at work
- Evidence needed: The achievement card mechanic creates a game structure. Social proof (real or anticipated) that the tattoos are funny enough to motivate application
- Mitigation: The achievement card is the adoption mechanism. It transforms "do you want to put on a tattoo?" (weak impulse) into "you survived that meeting - you earned this" (earned reward). The mix of small and medium sizes gives cautious recipients a low-commitment entry point

**Objection 3 - "Will the tattoos look good or cheap?"**
- Root cause: Bad temporary tattoo experiences with blurry prints, poor adhesion, or colors that look nothing like the preview
- Evidence needed: Close-up images of actual applied tattoos at realistic quality
- Mitigation: Print quality needs to be verified with samples. The designs should be clean-lined and bold enough to read well at temporary tattoo resolution. Overly detailed designs should be avoided - simple, graphic tattoo language works best at this format

**Objection 4 - "The designs might not be funny enough"**
- Root cause: If the buyer can see the tattoo designs in listing images and they don't land, the whole item feels like filler
- Evidence needed: Listing images showing 2-3 of the strongest designs with their copy pairings. The humor must be visible and immediate
- Mitigation: Lead with the strongest designs in listing photos. Choose designs where the image-and-copy pairing delivers the joke in under 2 seconds. Designs that require explanation are not strong enough for listing images

**Objection 5 - "This feels like filler to pad the kit's item count"**
- Root cause: If the tattoo sheet and box feel minimal compared to the pen, cards, and mug, buyers may perceive it as a thrown-in extra
- Evidence needed: The achievement card and premium box packaging need to communicate that this is a designed experience, not a bulk add-on
- Mitigation: The achievement card mechanic, the "Distinguished Honorees" back face, and the multi-surface box comedy all signal intentionality. This item has as much design thought as the pen - the packaging should make that visible
