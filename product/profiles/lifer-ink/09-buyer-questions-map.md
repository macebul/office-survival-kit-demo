# Artifact 9: Buyer Questions Map

Note: These questions are specific to the Lifer Ink item as it appears within the kit listing and unboxing experience.

**Questions the Buyer Asks When Evaluating the Listing**
- Temporary tattoos? For adults?
- What do the designs look like? (zooms in)
- Are these actually funny or are they generic office clip art?
- Would my coworker actually wear one of these?

**Questions the Recipient Asks During Unboxing**
- What's this gold box? (picks it up, drawn by the premium certificate aesthetic)
- Wait - temporary tattoos? (reads "LIFER INK" and connects to the prison theme)
- What does "BOLD. BRAVE. WASHABLE." mean? (opens the tuck flap)
- What's this card? (discovers achievement card, reads the commendation requirements)
- Which one should I do first? (scans the tattoo sheet for the funniest design)

**Questions the Group Asks While Watching**
- Are those tattoos? (leaning in to see the sheet)
- Read what they're for! (pointing at the achievement card)
- Which one are you going to get? (encouraging application)
- Do that one - you totally earned it! (peer pressure to apply)
- Let me see that card - I want to know which one I'd get (achievement card gets passed around)

**Questions Asked After Application**
- What's that on your arm? (coworker noticing the tattoo)
- Where did you get that? (the target question for social reach)
- Is there one for surviving [specific scenario]? (people project their own office complaints)
- Can I see the rest of them? (interest in the full set)
- Does the kit come with more? (interest extends to the full product)

**Questions the Buyer Asks Post-Purchase**
- Did they actually put one on? (the gift-giver's most hoped-for outcome)
- Which one did they pick? (curiosity about the recipient's choice)
- Did other people at the office want them too? (hoping for social proof)
