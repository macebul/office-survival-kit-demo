# Artifact 5: Buyer Personas

Note: Lifer Ink is not sold individually. These personas describe how each buyer type interacts with this specific item as part of the kit evaluation and unboxing experience.

## Persona 1 - "The Immediate Applier"

**Who**
- 22-35, any gender
- First to try anything new, first to volunteer for team-building activities
- Sees temporary tattoos and immediately asks "which one should I get?"

**Relationship to Lifer Ink**
- This person is the ideal recipient for this item
- They'll cut out a tattoo during the unboxing and apply it on the spot
- They're the reason the item generates social moments
- They turn the product from "thing in a box" into "event in a room"

**Key trigger**
- "Give me that - I'm doing this right now"

**Biggest fear**
- The tattoo designs aren't funny enough to be worth the bit

---

## Persona 2 - "The Achievement Hunter"

**Who**
- 25-40, any gender
- Responds to challenges, checklists, and completion mechanics
- The person who checks every box, fills every stamp card, completes every side quest

**Relationship to Lifer Ink**
- The achievement card is designed for this person
- They'll read the commendation requirements and start tracking which scenarios they've survived
- They'll apply tattoos as they "earn" them over days or weeks, extending the product's lifespan
- They may apply all of them eventually just to complete the set

**Key trigger**
- "Wait, you have to earn these? Okay, I've definitely done this one"

**Biggest fear**
- The requirements are too vague or too easy - the challenge has to feel real

---

## Persona 3 - "The Reluctant Participant"

**Who**
- 30-50, any gender
- Thinks temporary tattoos are for kids
- Needs social permission and peer pressure to actually apply one

**Relationship to Lifer Ink**
- This is the hardest persona to activate but the most rewarding when they convert
- The achievement card gives them an excuse: "I earned this one"
- Seeing a bolder coworker wearing one first lowers their barrier
- The premium packaging (cream/gold, corporate certificate) reframes the tattoos as adult and intentional

**Key trigger**
- "Fine. But only because I survived that exact meeting last Thursday"

**Biggest fear**
- Looking ridiculous or childish at work

---

## Persona 4 - "The Content Creator"

**Who**
- 22-40, any gender
- Documents everything, shares on social media, sends photos to group chats
- Sees every funny object as potential content

**Relationship to Lifer Ink**
- This person may or may not apply the tattoo, but they will photograph it
- They'll take a photo of the box, the achievement card, and the applied tattoo
- They'll share it on Instagram, TikTok, or the work Slack with the achievement requirement as caption
- They're the organic marketing engine for the kit

**Key trigger**
- "Oh this is going on my story immediately"

**Biggest fear**
- The tattoo designs don't photograph well or aren't visually legible in photos
