# Artifact 2: Strategic Overview

**Core Value Proposition**
- Temporary tattoos reframed as corporate commendations earned through office survival
- The prison tattoo parallel made literal: you earn your ink by surviving specific workplace scenarios
- Three-layer comedy: the object itself (temporary face tattoos for adults), Brad's framing (a legitimate professional achievement program), and the achievement card mechanic (turning application into a game)

**Strategic Position Within the Kit**
- The most physically interactive item in the kit after the pen
- While other items are read, held, or displayed, this one goes on your body
- The achievement card transforms a passive product (tattoo sheet you look at) into an active game (earn your ink, apply your commendation)
- Sits in Tray 1 alongside Cubicle Shank and Turf Tags, contributing to the "item discovery" phase

**Why This Item Matters**
- It's the item most likely to generate photos and social sharing
- A coworker wearing a temporary tattoo at work is inherently noticeable and conversation-starting
- The tattoos are designed to be funny on skin at conversational distance, meaning the joke travels with the person
- Each tattoo stands alone - a coworker who sees it doesn't need to know about the kit to get the joke

**Key Strategic Tension**
- Must feel hilarious and worth applying, not childish or juvenile
- The comedy comes from the corporate achievement framing, not from temporary tattoos being inherently funny
- If the tattoos look cheap or feel like kids' party favors, the "earned commendation" framing collapses
- If the designs are too subtle, nobody applies them and the product stays in the box unused
- Brad genuinely believes these are workplace-appropriate professional commendations - the gap between his sincerity and the absurdity is the comedy engine

**Box as Standalone Entertainment**
- The box uses premium corporate award certificate aesthetics (cream/ivory, gold accents) to frame temporary tattoos as executive recognition tools
- "Distinguished Honorees" concept on back face features corporate headshots of employees proudly displaying their commendation tattoos
- Every surface has a comedy beat: pharmaceutical warnings, laundry care symbols, institutional rubber stamps
- The "IF SWALLOWED" line on the bottom edge is potentially the single funniest line on any item box in the kit

**Revenue Contribution**
- Lower perceived standalone value than the pen ($2-4 mental price for temporary tattoos)
- But higher perceived comedy value - the laugh-per-dollar ratio is potentially the best in the kit
- The achievement card adds perceived value beyond "just tattoos" by making it a game system
- Social sharing potential creates secondary marketing value that other items don't generate
