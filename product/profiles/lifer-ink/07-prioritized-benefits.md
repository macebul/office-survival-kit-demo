# Artifact 7: Prioritized Benefits

Ranked by impact on the buyer's perception of the kit and the recipient's experience.

**Tier 1 - Must Communicate (drives social behavior and comedy impact)**
1. The tattoo designs are genuinely funny on skin at conversational distance - not generic novelty shop filler
2. Each tattoo represents a specific office survival achievement, making application feel earned rather than random
3. The achievement card transforms a passive tattoo sheet into an active social game
4. The box packaging is a comedy piece on its own, with the cream/gold corporate award aesthetic creating visual comedy before the box is opened

**Tier 2 - Should Communicate (builds engagement and extends value)**
5. Each tattoo stands alone - coworkers who see it don't need to know about the kit to get the joke
6. The "IF SWALLOWED, CONTACT YOUR SUPERVISOR" line demonstrates the humor style in a single sentence
7. The mix of small and medium sizes lets recipients choose their commitment level
8. "BOLD. BRAVE. WASHABLE." on the tuck flap frames the experience perfectly in three words

**Tier 3 - Nice to Communicate (differentiates for detail-oriented recipients)**
9. The laundry care symbols, pharmaceutical warnings, and institutional stamps add layers for those who examine every surface
10. The "Distinguished Honorees" back face concept (corporate headshots with visible tattoos) adds visual comedy
11. The achievement card's Brad Sterling voice creates consistency with every other item in the kit
12. The prison tattoo parallel grounds the designs in the kit's overarching conceit
