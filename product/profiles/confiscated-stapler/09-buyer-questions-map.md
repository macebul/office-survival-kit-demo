# Artifact 9: Buyer Questions Map

Note: These questions are specific to the Confiscated Stapler element as it appears within the kit listing and unboxing experience.

**Questions the buyer asks when evaluating the listing**
- How many items are actually in the kit?
- What's the "incident" they keep referencing?
- Is there really an empty slot or is that just marketing?
- Does the kit feel complete without a stapler?

**Questions the recipient asks during unboxing**
- Wait, why is this one empty? (opening Tray 2)
- Is that a chalk outline? OF A STAPLER? (recognizing the shape)
- What does this label say? (reading "CONFISCATED - WEAPONIZED OFFICE EQUIPMENT")
- What happened? What was Incident #147? (wanting the backstory)

**Questions the group asks while watching**
- What's in that slot? (seeing the recipient hesitate)
- Read what it says! (wanting to hear the label)
- Wait, there's NOTHING there? (processing the joke)
- Is there more to the story? (looking for the Activity Guide)

**Questions asked after unboxing**
- Did you see the empty stapler slot? (the detail people mention to others)
- What was Incident #147? (the question that has no official answer, which makes it funnier)
- Whose stapler was it? (projecting the joke onto their own office)

**Questions the buyer asks post-purchase**
- Did they find the chalk outline? (hoping the joke landed)
- Did they get the stapler reference? (wanting to know if the subtlety worked)
- Did they read the Activity Guide backstory? (wondering about deeper engagement)
