# Artifact 5: Buyer Personas

Note: The Confiscated Stapler is not a physical item. These personas describe how each buyer type interacts with the empty recess as part of the kit evaluation and unboxing experience.

## Persona 1 - "The Detail Discoverer"

**Who**
- 25-45, any gender
- Notices things other people miss
- Will explore every corner, read every label, flip every surface

**Relationship to the Confiscated Stapler**
- This is the person who spots the chalk outline before anyone else points it out
- They'll read the incident label aloud, look at the group, and wait for the reaction
- They'll connect Gary's exterior note to the empty recess and feel rewarded for paying attention
- The Activity Guide's incident report gives them backstory that nobody else in the room will have

**Key trigger**
- "Wait... there's nothing here. But look - there's a chalk outline. It's a STAPLER."

**Biggest fear**
- That nobody else in the group notices the empty recess and they have to explain the joke (explaining kills it)

---

## Persona 2 - "The Office Space Fan"

**Who**
- 30-50, any gender
- Has seen Office Space, understands the red stapler reference
- Appreciates workplace comedy that goes beyond surface-level jokes

**Relationship to the Confiscated Stapler**
- The stapler shape instantly triggers the Office Space association without the kit needing to make an explicit reference
- They appreciate the restraint - the joke works without naming the movie
- They'll tell the story as "there's a chalk outline where a stapler USED TO BE" and the reference sells itself
- This might be their single favorite detail in the entire kit

**Key trigger**
- "Oh my god, they did a chalk outline for a STAPLER" (the cultural reference clicks and enhances the visual)

**Biggest fear**
- None specific to this item - the empty recess can't disappoint someone who gets the reference

---

## Persona 3 - "The Value Counter"

**Who**
- 30-55, any gender
- Mentally inventories every item in the kit to assess value
- Notices when something is "missing" or doesn't match the item count

**Relationship to the Confiscated Stapler**
- Initially registers the empty recess as a negative - "wait, shouldn't there be something here?"
- The chalk outline and label convert the reaction from "this is missing" to "this is the joke"
- The conversion speed matters - if they feel cheated for more than 2 seconds before the joke lands, the comedy is undermined
- Once they get it, the zero-COGS insight actually impresses them - clever design that adds value without adding cost

**Key trigger**
- "Oh wait - it was CONFISCATED" (the label reframes the absence from deficiency to comedy)

**Biggest fear**
- That the empty recess is actually a mistake or cost-cutting measure disguised as a joke

---

## Persona 4 - "The Storyteller"

**Who**
- 25-50, any gender
- Retells the best moments from gifts, experiences, and events
- Collects details that make stories better

**Relationship to the Confiscated Stapler**
- The empty recess becomes the anchor of their unboxing story
- "And then we opened the second tray and there was a CHALK OUTLINE where a STAPLER should have been" is a story beat that consistently gets a reaction
- The incident label gives them a punchline: "CONFISCATED - WEAPONIZED OFFICE EQUIPMENT"
- They'll retell this detail to people who weren't there, and it sells the kit concept in one sentence

**Key trigger**
- "You have to see this" (pulling someone over to look at the empty recess)

**Biggest fear**
- That the story doesn't land as well in retelling as it did in person (the visual component is important)
