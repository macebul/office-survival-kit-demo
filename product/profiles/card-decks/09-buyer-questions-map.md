# Artifact 9: Buyer Questions Map

**Questions the buyer asks when evaluating the listing**
- What do the cards actually say?
- Are there three different card games or three sets of the same thing?
- How many cards in each deck?
- Are the cards funny enough for adults or are they generic?
- Could I see myself giving these to a coworker?

**Questions the recipient asks during unboxing**
- Wait, there are THREE card decks? (volume surprise)
- What's the difference between them? (reads box descriptions)
- Okay read me one of the affirmation ones (picks Cell Talk)
- Oh my god, read the back (discovers Brad's commentary)
- Which one's the mean one? (reaches for Sweet Nothings)
- This one's going on Dave's desk Monday (identifies target for Rap Sheets)

**Questions the group asks while watching**
- Read another one! (wanting more content after the first card)
- Do they all have something on the back? (discovering the dual-sided mechanic)
- Which one is the best? (comparing deck favorites)
- Can I see the passive-aggressive ones? (reaching for Sweet Nothings)

**Questions asked after unboxing (days/weeks later)**
- Which card deck was the mean one again? (returning to deploy cards)
- Did you leave that card on my desk? (coworker discovering a deployed Sweet Nothing)
- Where can I get these? (coworker who received a card wanting the full kit)

**Questions the buyer asks post-purchase**
- Did they like the cards? (checking which deck was the favorite)
- Have they been using them at work? (hoping for ongoing deployment)
- Does this company make other card sets? (interest in expanding the collection)
