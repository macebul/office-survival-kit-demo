# Artifact 7: Prioritized Benefits

Ranked by impact on the buyer's purchase decision and the recipient's ongoing experience.

**Tier 1 - Must Communicate (drives perceived value and humor confidence)**
1. Three distinct card decks with different themes create visible density and variety in the kit
2. The card content is genuinely funny and relatable to anyone who has worked in an office
3. Cards are interactive and designed for deployment to coworkers, extending the gift's life well beyond unboxing
4. Each deck has its own visual identity, so three boxes feel like three products rather than padding

**Tier 2 - Should Communicate (builds engagement and anticipation)**
5. Every card has a front/back reveal mechanic that creates a physical comedy moment
6. The three humor styles (wellness parody, bureaucratic parody, luxury stationery parody) cover different moods and situations
7. Brad Sterling's character deepens across all 45 cards, building a comedic relationship with the reader
8. The cards are designed to be read aloud, making them group entertainment during unboxing

**Tier 3 - Nice to Communicate (differentiates for detail-oriented buyers)**
9. Each deck has its own Elevate Co. division with a unique division name and Brad role
10. Card content is original comedy writing, not recycled internet humor or stock phrases
11. The passive-aggressive deck (Sweet Nothings) uses craft-based insults built around everyday objects, making them funny rather than mean
12. The three distinct box aesthetics (zen green, enforcement black, luxury pink) photograph well together as a collection
