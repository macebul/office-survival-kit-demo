# Artifact 5: Buyer Personas

Note: The card decks are not sold individually. These personas describe how each buyer type interacts with these items as part of the kit evaluation and unboxing experience.

## Persona 1 - "The Card Deployer"

**Who**
- 25-40, any gender
- The person who will actually use every card in the deck
- Treats the office as a stage and the cards as props

**Relationship to the Card Decks**
- This is their favorite part of the kit
- They'll have a Rap Sheet on Dave's desk by Monday morning
- They'll leave Sweet Nothings on the printer for discovery
- Cell Talk becomes their ironic morning ritual

**Key trigger**
- "Oh my god, this one is PERFECT for [specific coworker]"

**Biggest fear**
- The cards are generic and don't match real office situations

---

## Persona 2 - "The Content Browser"

**Who**
- 30-50, any gender
- Reads through all 45 cards in one sitting during the unboxing
- Appreciates the writing craft more than the deployment potential

**Relationship to the Card Decks**
- They'll read every card and rank their favorites
- They care about consistency - are all 15 cards in a deck actually good, or do they fall off after 5?
- They're evaluating the comedy writing, not just the concept

**Key trigger**
- "Every single one of these is funny - there are no filler cards"

**Biggest fear**
- Half the cards are mediocre and only 3-4 are actually quotable

---

## Persona 3 - "The Social Sharer"

**Who**
- 25-35, social media active
- Will photograph their favorite cards and post them
- Sees the cards as content, not just a gift

**Relationship to the Card Decks**
- Sweet Nothings is their favorite deck because the front/back reveal is most shareable
- They'll photograph individual cards flat-lay style with the box
- They'll text cards to friends who weren't at the unboxing

**Key trigger**
- "I have to post this one" (photographs a specific card)

**Biggest fear**
- The cards don't photograph well or the text is too small to read in photos

---

## Persona 4 - "The Deck Collector"

**Who**
- 28-45, any gender
- Appreciates the three-deck system as a designed collection
- Notices the distinct aesthetics and branding across decks

**Relationship to the Card Decks**
- They appreciate that each deck has its own visual identity, division name, and Brad role
- The boxes become display items on their desk shelf
- They may organize cards by favorites across all three decks

**Key trigger**
- "These are three completely different products. Each one has its own whole look"

**Biggest fear**
- The decks all look the same and feel repetitive
