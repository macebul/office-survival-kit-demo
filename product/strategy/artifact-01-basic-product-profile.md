## Key Features
- 8 individually packaged items in a single kit
- Sardonic office-is-prison humor throughout - the office IS a prison, never acknowledged as metaphor
- Warm, cheerful, conspiratorial brand voice using institutional formats (FDA labels, QC stamps, comparison charts, evidence tags) filled with escalating dark humor
- Comedy writing dense enough to reward re-reading - scan for one laugh, close read for escalating laughs
- Three card decks function as social tools - designed to be handed to coworkers, left on desks, read aloud
- Pen and mug provide real daily-use utility beyond the humor
- Multiple comedy surfaces per item (card front, card back, evidence tags)
- Hidden Easter eggs reward curious recipients
- Recurring characters woven throughout: Kevin (coworker villain, 4-6 references), Gary (quality inspector, stamps/badges only, 2-3 references), Diane from HR (implied through policy language, never named, 1-2 references)
- "Normal, normal, crazy" escalation pattern - format stays institutional, content goes off the rails
- Clean/sardonic humor - mocks systems and behaviors, not individuals - safe for workplace gift exchanges

## Brief Differentiator
Unlike the ziplock bags of candy and dollar-store trinkets that dominate Amazon's "office survival kit" category, this kit delivers actual production quality (enamel mug, aluminum pen), professional comedy writing dense enough to quote and screenshot, and items with real daily utility that live on a desk rather than in a drawer. It sits in an underserved price tier ($25-30) between cheap single-item gag gifts and premium gift baskets.
