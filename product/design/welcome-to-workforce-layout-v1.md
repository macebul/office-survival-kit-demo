## Overview

This is a 4-page feature that spans three distinct visual zones:

1. **Pages 4-5 (spread):** Feature article opener — hero image left, headline + body text right
2. **Page 6 (single):** Editorial continuation — modular layout with schedule strip, photo, callouts
3. **Page 7 (single):** Hard format break — real estate listing (completely different visual energy)

All 4 pages use the B-Feature Spread master page (cream background, running headers, page numbers). The 6-column grid and 15pt baseline grid apply throughout.

### Schedule Formatting Notes

The schedule is NOT a wall of text. It's a visual element — a condensed daily planner. Format options:

**Option A (recommended): Two-column table**
- Left column: Time (IBM Plex Mono Medium, Warm Gray 80, right-aligned)
- Right column: Activity (Freight Text Pro Book, Charcoal, left-aligned)
- Alternating row backgrounds: none / Warm Gray 15%
- Horizontal rule between each row (0.25pt, Warm Gray 40)
- The table occupies the top ~35% of the live area

**Option B: Timeline strip**
- Vertical timeline down the left margin (2pt Gold rule)
- Time markers as nodes on the timeline
- Activity text beside each node
- More dynamic but harder to fit 14 entries

## Density & Pacing Notes

| Page | Density | Speed | Reader Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Medium | Medium | Image-dominant. The hero orientation photo catches the eye. The badge close-up and Brad sidebar are secondary entry points. |
| 5 | Medium | Slow | Text-dominant. The headline lands first, then the body copy. Two-column text flow keeps it readable. The 3-line drop cap signals "start reading here." |
| 6 | High | Fast | Modular. Three distinct visual blocks. The schedule is scannable (each entry is its own micro-joke). The flat-lay photo provides breathing room. The callout box is the kicker. No block requires sustained reading. |
| 7 | High | Fast | Format break. The Zillow listing is instant recognition. The specs table and related properties are scannable. Every element is a comedy beat. The reader is laughing at format, not reading prose. |

**Transition from p. 7 to p. 8:** Hard cut. Page 7 is cream background, real estate listing, warm and inviting. Page 8 is the Cubicle Shank ad — dark background, product photography, QVC energy. The contrast is the rhythm. The reader flips from "welcome to your cubicle" to "here's how to weaponize a pen." The tone shift is intentional.
