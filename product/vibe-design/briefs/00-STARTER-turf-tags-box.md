## MY PROJECT

I need a Design Brief for the **Turf Tags item box** — the individual packaging for the stickers inside the Office Survival Kit gag gift.

### What This Surface Is

A small tuck-end box that holds a set of stickers. It sits in Tray 1 of the briefcase, in the wide back recess alongside the Lifer Ink box.

### Physical Specs

- **Box type:** Tuck-end
- **Box dimensions:** ~5" x 3.5" x 0.5" (WORKING ESTIMATE)
- **Location:** Tray 1, wide recess (back), alongside Lifer Ink
- **Print:** Full color exterior, Brad's corporate branding
- **Format:** Sticker sheet packaging

### Item Inside

- **Item name:** Turf Tags
- **Brad's rebrand:** Workspace Personalization Labels™
- **What it is:** Stickers — office-themed stickers for marking territory, personalizing workspace, or leaving messages
- **Item description:** Claim your space. Mark your territory. Let everyone know which corner of the open floor plan you've decided is yours.

### Brand Context

**The World:** The office is a prison — played straight. "Turf" = your desk, your claimed spaces (territorial tone). "Tags" = graffiti, marking territory. Stickers as the office equivalent of tagging your cell.

**Character:** Brad Sterling (B.S.) — sole voice. Two modes: (1) competent product copy, (2) delusional personal branding.

**Comedy rule:** Format stays buttoned-up, content gets unleashed.

**Tone:** Sardonic, not cynical. Warm.

### The Item Box's Role in the Experience

- Discovered when Tray 1 is revealed (after lifting Doc Set 1) — first layer of items the recipient sees
- Part of the Pelican-case reveal moment — items sitting in die-cut recesses
- Sits alongside Lifer Ink in the wide recess; both are flat, similarly sized boxes
- Must work as a standalone funny object
- Stickers are the most interactive/shareable item — people might stick them on things in the office immediately

### Design Consideration

Turf Tags and Lifer Ink share the same recess area in Tray 1 and have similar dimensions. They should feel related but distinct — stickers vs. tattoos, workspace marking vs. body art. Consider how these two flat-format boxes relate visually.

### Type of Project

- A. Packaging

### Complexity

- B. Moderate
