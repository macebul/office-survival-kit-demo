## MY PROJECT

I need a Design Brief for the **compartment cover** — the J-shaped interior surface that conceals the hidden mug compartment inside the Office Survival Kit gag gift.

### What This Surface Is

A rigid paperboard J-shaped piece that serves triple duty: (1) its flat top surface is a platform for Doc Set 2, (2) its vertical front surface acts as the divider wall between the tray zone and mug compartment, (3) its underside is a printable narrative surface. It lifts off to reveal the hidden mug compartment — the kit's final surprise.

### Physical Specs

- **Material:** Rigid paperboard (~0.0625" thick)
- **Shape:** J-shaped (flat top + vertical front wall)
- **Top surface dimensions:** ~13.5" x ~4.0" (WORKING ESTIMATE)
- **Vertical surface:** Serves as divider wall between front zone (trays) and back zone (mug compartment)
- **Both sides printable** (top and underside of flat section, plus vertical surface)
- **Mechanism:** Lift-off lid — lifts up and out

### Printable Surfaces (3 distinct areas)

| Surface | What It Does | When It's Seen |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Top (flat, facing up) | Platform for Doc Set 2 + teaser copy | After lifting Tray 2 |
| Vertical front wall | Visible between trays — divider wall | During tray reveals (Tray 1 and Tray 2 visible) |
| Underside (flat, facing down) | Narrative beat — revealed only when cover is lifted | The moment before the hidden mug is discovered |

### Brand Context

**The World:** The office is a prison — played straight. The hidden compartment is contraband storage. The cover is what conceals the contraband.

**Character:** Brad Sterling (B.S.) — sole voice. Two modes: (1) competent product copy, (2) delusional personal branding.

**Comedy rule:** Format stays buttoned-up, content gets unleashed.

**Tone:** Sardonic, not cynical. Warm.

### The Compartment Cover's Role in the Experience

- The vertical front wall is visible throughout the tray reveals — it's ambient/background during Tray 1 and Tray 2
- The top surface is discovered after lifting Tray 2 — it holds Doc Set 2 and any teaser copy
- The underside is the LAST thing the recipient sees before discovering the hidden mug compartment
- This is a pivotal narrative moment — the underside copy should build anticipation or deliver a comedic beat that makes lifting the cover feel like a reveal
- The transition from "teaser on top" to "reveal underneath" to "hidden mug" is the kit's climactic sequence

### Design Consideration

This surface is unique because it has three distinct printable areas that are seen at different moments in the experience. The vertical wall is visible early and often (background during tray reveals). The top is seen mid-experience. The underside is the penultimate reveal. Each surface needs to work for its moment while feeling like one cohesive piece. The underside is especially important — it's the last thing between the recipient and the hidden compartment.

### Type of Project

- A. Packaging (interior surface)

### Complexity

- C. Complex/significant (3 printable surfaces, narrative sequencing, pivotal reveal moment)
