## MY PROJECT

I need a Design Brief for the **Lifer Ink item box** — the individual packaging for the temporary tattoos inside the Office Survival Kit gag gift.

### What This Surface Is

A small tuck-end box that holds a set of temporary tattoos. It sits in Tray 1 of the briefcase, in the wide back recess alongside the Turf Tags box.

### Physical Specs

- **Box type:** Tuck-end
- **Box dimensions:** ~5" x 3.5" x 0.5" (WORKING ESTIMATE)
- **Location:** Tray 1, wide recess (back), alongside Turf Tags
- **Print:** Full color exterior, Brad's corporate branding
- **Format:** Tattoo sheet packaging

### Item Inside

- **Item name:** Lifer Ink
- **Brad's rebrand:** Motivational Body Art Collection™
- **What it is:** Temporary tattoos — subtle, universally enjoyable, office-wearable designs (LOCKED direction)
- **Item description:** Marks of time served. Earned, not given. Apply to visible skin and let your colleagues know you're not going anywhere.

### Brand Context

**The World:** The office is a prison — played straight. "Lifer" = long-tenured employee (respectful tone). "Ink" = tattoos marking time served (earned tone). Prison tattoos as office loyalty badges.

**Character:** Brad Sterling (B.S.) — sole voice. Two modes: (1) competent product copy, (2) delusional personal branding.

**Comedy rule:** Format stays buttoned-up, content gets unleashed.

**Tone:** Sardonic, not cynical. Warm.

### The Item Box's Role in the Experience

- Discovered when Tray 1 is revealed (after lifting Doc Set 1) — first layer of items the recipient sees
- Part of the Pelican-case reveal moment — items sitting in die-cut recesses
- Sits alongside Turf Tags in the wide recess; both are flat, similarly sized boxes
- Must work as a standalone funny object
- The tattoo concept bridges the prison metaphor (prison tattoos) with office culture (loyalty, tenure)

### Design Consideration

Lifer Ink and Turf Tags share the same recess area in Tray 1 and have similar dimensions. They should feel related but distinct — tattoos vs. stickers, body art vs. workspace marking. Consider how these two flat-format boxes relate visually.

### Type of Project

- A. Packaging

### Complexity

- B. Moderate
