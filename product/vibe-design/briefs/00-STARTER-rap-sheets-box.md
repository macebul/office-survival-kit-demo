## MY PROJECT

I need a Design Brief for the **Rap Sheets item box** — the individual packaging for the complaint cards inside the Office Survival Kit gag gift.

### What This Surface Is

A small tuck-end box that holds a deck of complaint cards. It sits in Tray 2 of the briefcase, in the wide back recess alongside two other card deck boxes (Cell Talk and Sweet Nothings).

### Physical Specs

- **Box type:** Tuck-end
- **Box dimensions:** ~4" x 2.5" x 0.75" (WORKING ESTIMATE)
- **Location:** Tray 2, wide recess (back), alongside Cell Talk and Sweet Nothings
- **Print:** Full color exterior, Brad's corporate branding
- **Format:** Business-card deck format

### Item Inside

- **Item name:** Rap Sheets
- **Brad's rebrand:** Interpersonal Feedback Delivery System™
- **What it is:** Complaint cards — pre-written complaint/feedback forms reframed through the office-as-prison lens
- **Item description:** Formal documentation for the things your coworkers do. Because verbal warnings never worked.

### Brand Context

**The World:** The office is a prison — played straight. "Rap Sheet" = your record, your documented history of offenses. Here it's repurposed as complaint cards you fill out about coworkers.

**Character:** Brad Sterling (B.S.) — sole voice. Two modes: (1) competent product copy, (2) delusional personal branding.

**Comedy rule:** Format stays buttoned-up, content gets unleashed.

**Tone:** Sardonic, not cynical. Warm.

### The Item Box's Role in the Experience

- Discovered when Tray 2 is revealed (after lifting Tray 1)
- Sits alongside Cell Talk and Sweet Nothings — the three card decks form a visual set
- Must be visually cohesive with the other two card deck boxes while having its own identity
- Must work as a standalone funny object
- This is the "negative/complaint" card deck — the counterpoint to Cell Talk's positivity and Sweet Nothings' passive-aggression

### Design Consideration

The three Tray 2 card boxes (Cell Talk, Rap Sheets, Sweet Nothings) share identical dimensions and sit side by side. They should feel like a cohesive set — like a matched series — while each having a distinct personality. The system should already be established in the Cell Talk brief; this brief should extend it.

### Type of Project

- A. Packaging

### Complexity

- B. Moderate
