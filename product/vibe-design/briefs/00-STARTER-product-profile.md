## 2. Product Description
**Purpose:** Core narrative that explains what the product IS in both literal and conceptual terms
**Include:**
- One paragraph literal description (what's physically in it)
- One paragraph conceptual description (what experience it creates)
- The transformation it promises (from what state to what state)
- Product Category Positioning
- Primary use case scenario
- The Purchase Decision

---

## 3. Physical Contents/Specifications
**Purpose:** Detailed inventory for accurate product descriptions and setting expectations
**Include:**
- Complete list of all included items with quantities
- Descriptive details for each item (size, material, quality indicators)
- Packaging description (box, tin, bag, etc.)
- Dimensions and weight if relevant
- Any special features or quality points

---

## 4. What Customers Are REALLY Buying
**Purpose:** The psychological and emotional value beyond the physical product
**Potentially Include:**
- **Surface Purchase:** What it appears to be
- **Actual Purchase:** The emotional/social needs it fulfills
  - Permission to feel/express something
  - Social currency or status
  - Relationship dynamics it creates
  - Memories or experiences it generates
  - Problems it solves beyond its literal function
  - Deeper Purchase Motivations
- **Success Metrics:** What a "win" looks like for the buyer

---

## 5. Customer Pain Points & Solutions
**Purpose:** Specific problems this product solves that drive purchase decisions
**Structure each pain point as:**
- **The Problem:** Specific situation causing frustration
- **Why It's Hard:** What makes this problem persistent
- **How This Solves It:** Specific mechanism of relief
- **What They've Tried Before:** Failed alternatives

**Essential Pain Points to Address:**
- Shopping/gift-giving challenges
- Social/relationship dynamics
- Decision-Making Context
- Emotional expression barriers
- Situational dilemmas (occasions, obligations)
- Identity/self-expression needs

---

## 6. Target Audience
**Purpose:** Clear picture of who buys and who receives, enabling targeted content
**Include:**
- **Primary Purchaser:** Demographics, psychographics, shopping behavior
- **Primary Recipient:** If different from purchaser
- **Purchase Occasions:** Specific events/seasons/triggers
- **Personality Indicators:** Humor style, values, lifestyle
- **Exclusions:** Who this is NOT for (equally important)

---

## 7. Key Messaging & Language
**Purpose:** Actual words and phrases LLMs should use or reference
**Include:**
- Brand taglines and slogans
- Product-specific phrases and wordplay
- Tone descriptors (e.g., "military-tactical", "irreverent")
- Vocabulary themes (technical terms, slang, jargon)
- Phrases to avoid (what doesn't resonate)
- Power words that trigger action

---

## 8. Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)
**Purpose:** Clear differentiation points for competitive positioning
**Include:**
- What makes this different from alternatives
- Specific features only this product has
- Quality or value advantages
- Brand-specific benefits
- "Only" statements (Only gift that...)
- Defensive positioning against objections

These are the ONLY sections to include. Don't include any more unless the user specifically asks you.
