### B7 -- Application Instructions (7-Step)
```
APPLICATION PROCEDURE

1. CUT -- Select your commendation. Carefully.
2. REMOVE -- Peel the clear film. Don't peel anything else.
3. PLACE -- Apply image-side down on skin. Clean skin.
   Yours or someone else's -- your call.
4. APPLY -- Press with a wet cloth for 30 seconds.
   Like CPR, but for your reputation.
5. PEEL -- Slide off the backing. Admire yourself.
   You've earned this.
6. ALLOW -- Let dry for 60 seconds. Resist the urge to
   touch it. Or explain it.
7. DISPLAY -- Wear it to the Monday stand-up.
   Maintain eye contact.
```
**Notes:** Institutional manual style. Each step has a functional directive + editorial commentary. Left side, 55% width. Largest content block on back.
