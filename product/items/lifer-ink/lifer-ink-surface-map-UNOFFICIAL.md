## DESIGN STYLE REFERENCE

Based on the Cubicle Shank front surface mockup (v01):
- **Background:** Dark/black
- **Accent:** Red border lines, red for tagline/subline text
- **Product name:** Large white condensed sans-serif (bold, all caps)
- **Brand block:** Smaller white caps, spaced
- **Tagline:** Red italic serif or script
- **Supporting copy:** Small white sans-serif
- **Seal/badge:** Red ribbon-style badge element in corner

Lifer Ink should follow the same system for brand consistency across the kit, with adjustments appropriate to the tattoo product (e.g., the seal is "DERMATOLOGIST RECOMMENDED" instead of "GOOD SHANKING SEAL OF APPROVAL").
