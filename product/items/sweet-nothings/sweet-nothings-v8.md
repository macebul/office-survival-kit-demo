# Sweet Nothings — Full Deck Draft v8
## Ambient Communication Cards™

**Format:** 4" × 2.5"
**Front:** Something genuinely nice.
**Back:** Rare insult. So weirdly specific and absurd that the craft of the insult is funnier than the insult itself. Nobody's feelings get hurt because the image is too bizarre to take personally.

# Deck Assessment

## What Changed
Every insult is now built around a hyper-specific, absurd everyday image — shopping cart wheels, Roombas, expired coupons, IKEA furniture, bananas. The comparisons are so weirdly precise that the CRAFT of the insult is what's funny, not the meanness. You can't take "you have the leadership qualities of the front shopping cart wheel" personally because you're too busy laughing at how perfect the comparison is.

## Strongest Candidates
- **Card 6** — "Claps when the plane lands and then tries to start a conversation about it." Everyone knows this person. The image is so specific it hurts.
- **Card 8** — The shopping cart wheel. Universally experienced, immediately understood, the "goes sideways while the other three try to move forward" is perfect.
- **Card 9** — IKEA furniture with extra parts. "I don't know what you're for but throwing you away feels risky" is the rare insult sweet spot.
- **Card 12** — "Getting directions from a dog. You seem very sure. You are pointing at a squirrel." Simple, stupid, visual.
- **Card 15** — The banana. "Confidently. Publicly. With spots." The rhythm alone is funny.

## What I'm Less Sure About
- **Card 2** — Check engine light might be running long for the card format.
- **Card 7** — Roomba is a good image but the back might have one beat too many.
- **Card 14** — Software update comparison is solid but might be heading toward "too clever" territory you flagged earlier.
