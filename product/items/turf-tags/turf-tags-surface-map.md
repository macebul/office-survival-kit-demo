# Turf Tags — Surface Map (Complete Copy Specification)

**Last Updated:** July 6, 2026
**Purpose:** Every word of copy that goes on the Turf Tags tuck-end box, tray card front, and tray card back, with exact surface placement. This document is self-contained — no other document is needed to execute the design.
**Box dimensions:** ~5.0" wide × 3.5" tall × 0.5" deep (tuck-end box)
**Box material:** Paperboard, bold yellow background with black type throughout
**Tray Card dimensions:** 13.25" wide × 10.75" tall, L-shaped (4.75" × 3.75" notch cut from upper-left corner to accommodate the Mug Shot box)
**Tray Card material:** 18pt matte black paperboard, printed both sides
**Product:** Vinyl sticker sheet (~6.5" × 4.5", die-cut, 8 stickers)
**Brand:** Witty Yeti
**Division:** D.I.B.S. — Department of Individual Boundary Security

## SURFACE 1: BOX FRONT (5.0" × 3.5")

**Job:** Sell the concept in one glance. Bold yellow background, black type. The dog illustration does half the work before the copy even registers. Everything above the red divider is identity and hook. Everything below is fake compliance.

### Element 1: Brand Header

```
WITTY YETI
```

**Placement:** Top left, above everything.

### Element 2: Division Name + Tagline

```
D.I.B.S. — Department of Individual Boundary Security
Protecting What's Yours Because You Said So.
```

**Placement:** Below brand header. Division name in spaced uppercase. Tagline subordinate, smaller type.

**Design note:** "D.I.B.S." reads as the word "dibs" — the childhood claim-staking call. The full expansion sounds like a real government agency. The tagline acknowledges that the only authority behind any of this is the user's declaration. Played straight.

### Element 3: Product Name (hero text)

```
TURF TAGS™
```

**Placement:** Upper portion, spanning most of the width. Dominant scale. The biggest text on the front face.

### Element 4: Tagline

```
MARK YOUR TERRITORY.
```

**Placement:** Adjacent to or above the dog illustration.

### Element 5: Hero Illustration

**Description:** Black silhouette of a dog lifting its leg on an office chair. Clean, bold, reads instantly at any distance.

**Placement:** Center of front face. The visual anchor of the entire box.

**Design note:** The dog silhouette communicates the entire concept without a word of copy. Territory marking is primal. The silhouette style keeps it tasteful and graphically bold — it reads as an icon, not a cartoon. The yellow background makes the black silhouette pop.

### Element 6: Subtitle

```
Workspace Personalization Labels™
```

**Placement:** Below "TURF TAGS™". Smaller, lighter weight.

**Design note:** The corporate translation of "territory-marking stickers." The gap between "TURF TAGS" and "Workspace Personalization Labels" is the same product in two languages — primal on top, sanitized underneath.

### Element 7: Companion Line

```
Because Asking Nicely Means Putting Down Your Shank.
```

**Placement:** Below subtitle. Italic or lighter weight.

**Design note:** Explains why the product exists — the diplomatic phase is over. Cross-references the Cubicle Shank, another product in the kit. The reader who has already opened the Shank gets an Easter egg. The reader who hasn't just thinks it's funny.

### Element 8: Red Divider Line

**Description:** A horizontal red line separating the identity zone (above) from the compliance zone (below).

**Placement:** Spanning the width, roughly two-thirds down.

### Element 9: QTY Line

```
Contains: 8 Adhesive Ultimatums. Down From 100. Legal Cut the Personal Attacks.
```

**Placement:** Below red divider, left-aligned. Small, institutional text.

**Design note:** "Adhesive Ultimatums" is the regulatory term for stickers. "Down From 100" implies the original count was absurd. "Legal Cut the Personal Attacks" implies some stickers were so targeted they constituted individual harassment and had to be removed by legal review. The format is a contents declaration. The content is a production history.

### Element 10: Fine Print

```
FOR SURFACE APPLICATION ONLY*

*Anything that holds still long enough.
```

**Placement:** Below QTY line. The asterisk and footnote should be visually connected but the footnote in smaller text.

**Design note:** "For surface application only" reads as a standard product instruction. The footnote redefines "surface" to include anything — or anyone — that isn't moving. The implication that these have been applied to things that were moving is the joke.

### Front Face — Reading Order

Brand → Division → Product Name → Tagline → Hero Illustration → Subtitle → Companion Line → [Red Divider] → QTY Line → Fine Print

## SURFACE 2: BOX BACK (5.0" × 3.5")

**Job:** Sticker preview, product description, and age recommendation. Clean layout — let the sticker designs and the three-beat product description carry the surface.

### Element 1: Sticker Preview Grid

**Description:** 8 sticker design thumbnails arranged in a grid layout. Each sticker shown as a small icon/preview at reduced scale. The designs are:

| # | Image | Text on Sticker |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dog silhouette lifting leg | MARKED. |
| 2 | Three diagonal claw marks | PET FRIENDLY. |
| 3 | Squirrel on pile of office supplies | WINTER'S COMING. |
| 4 | Skunk with raised tail | FINAL WARNING |
| 5 | Cat pushing object off desk | RESTRUCTURING. |
| 6 | Skull with pen shank | PREVIOUS OWNER. |
| 7 | Three coffins (casket, filing cabinet, Viking boat) | HELP YOURSELF. OPTIONS AVAILABLE. |
| 8 | Four-panel IKEA pictogram (grab, catch, launch, memorial) | FYI. FOR REFERENCE. |

**Placement:** Upper two-thirds of the back face.

**Design note:** The preview grid lets the recipient see what's inside before opening. The sticker images should be large enough to identify the illustrations but small enough to fit all 8. Stickers 7 and 8 are horizontal strips and may need a different grid position than the smaller die-cut stickers.

### Element 2: Product Description + Icons

```
Your stuff. Our stickers. Their problem.
```

**Placement:** Below sticker grid. Three icons above the three text beats:

| Icon | Text Below |
|---|---|
| Desk with sticker applied | Your stuff. |
| Hand peeling sticker from backing | Our stickers. |
| Stick figure in body cast | Their problem. |

**Design note:** Icons in the same IKEA-flat line art style used on Long Side A. Each icon illustrates one beat of the product description. The third icon escalates — "their problem" is illustrated by medical consequences. The icons should be evenly spaced in a horizontal row.

### Element 3: Age Recommendation

```
Suitable for Anyone With Enemies.
```

**Placement:** Bottom edge of back face. Small text, centered.

## SURFACES 3–7: BOX SIDES & TUCK FLAP

### Surface 3: Long Side A — Top Edge (5.0" × 0.5")

**Job:** The standout surface. Five IKEA-style instruction icons in a deployment procedure strip.

```
1. PEEL → 2. STICK → 3. HOLD COURT → 4. ACCEPT PARADE → 5. EXIT
```

| Step | Icon Description |
|---|---|
| 1. PEEL | Hand peeling corner of a sticker from backing sheet |
| 2. STICK | Hand pressing sticker flat onto a surface, fingers splayed |
| 3. HOLD COURT | Figure seated on throne, two smaller figures bowing — medieval royal audience in IKEA-flat style |
| 4. ACCEPT PARADE | Figure standing in convertible, waving to crowd, confetti — ticker-tape parade in IKEA-flat style |
| 5. EXIT | Figure climbing rope ladder to helicopter departing from office building roof — dramatic extraction in IKEA-flat style |

**Design note:** Steps 1–2 are real instructions. Steps 3–5 escalate into absurdity. The IKEA instruction format is universally recognized — using it for sticker deployment is already funny. The escalation from "press sticker onto surface" to "helicopter extraction" is what makes it exceptional. Icons must be simple enough to read at 0.5" height but detailed enough that throne, convertible, and helicopter are identifiable. Sequential left-to-right with thin vertical dividers between panels. Numbered steps in small text above each icon.

### Surface 4: Long Side B — Bottom Edge (5.0" × 0.5")

**Job:** Discovery surface. Product warning with stick-figure icon.

```
NOT RECOMMENDED ON INTERNS. THEY BITE.
```

**Icon:** Small IKEA-flat stick figure snapping/biting at a hand attempting to apply a sticker. Same visual language as Long Side A icons.

**Placement:** Centered on the strip with icon to the left of the text.

**Design note:** "Not recommended on" reads like a surface compatibility warning ("not recommended on leather," "not recommended on painted walls"). The surface is a person. The reason isn't product damage — the surface bites back. The icon makes it an instructional diagram.

### Surface 5: Short Side A (3.5" × 0.5")

**Job:** Thesis statement.

```
SHARING IS ~~CARING~~ OVER.
```

**Design note:** "Sharing is caring" is a childhood platitude. The strikethrough kills "caring" and replaces it with "over." The entire territorial philosophy compressed into one visual edit. The strikethrough is the design element — it must be clearly visible as a struck-through word, not just replaced text.

### Surface 6: Short Side B (3.5" × 0.5")

**Job:** Packaging format subverted.

```
SHELF LIFE: PERMANENT. LIKE THE RESENTMENT.
```

**Design note:** "Shelf life:" is real packaging language. "Permanent" ties to the stickers (vinyl, hard to remove) and to grudges (they don't go away either). The second sentence recontextualizes the first — the product's shelf life and the user's resentment are both permanent.

### Surface 7: Tuck Flap (interior, ~3.5" × ~1")

**Job:** First thing seen when box opens. A two-word command.

```
PEEL RESPONSIBLY.
```

**Design note:** "Drink responsibly" applied to stickers. Implies that irresponsible peeling is absolutely what's about to happen. Clean, centered, bold black text on the inside of the tuck flap.

## SURFACE 8: TRAY CARD FRONT

**Job:** Feature selling surface. The card is visible in the briefcase tray — this is where feature bullets, the sticker slap element, the badge, and the accent tagline live.

### Element 1: Brand Header

```
WITTY YETI
```

**Placement:** Top of card, near hang hole area.

### Element 2: Division Name

```
D.I.B.S. — Department of Individual Boundary Security
Protecting What's Yours Because You Said So.
```

**Placement:** Below brand header.

### Element 3: Product Name

```
TURF TAGS™
```

**Placement:** Hero headline area. Large, bold.

### Element 4: Subtitle

```
Workspace Personalization Labels™
```

**Placement:** Below product name, smaller.

### Element 5: Feature Bullets

```
• Vinyl construction rated for indoor, outdoor, and passive-aggressive use.
• Adhesive tested on 14 surfaces. 3 were willing.
• Won't fade, peel, or take a hint.
```

**Placement:** Below subtitle area. Standard bullet list format.

**Design note:** Each bullet follows the same pattern — real product spec language that goes sideways on the third beat. "Passive-aggressive use" is a product application category. "3 were willing" implies the other 11 surfaces were stickered without consent. "Take a hint" personifies the sticker as someone who won't leave.

### Element 6: Sticker Slap — Application Technique

```
CAN'T SLAP A COWORKER. CAN APPLY A STICKER WITH ENTHUSIASM.
LEGAL SAYS THERE'S A DIFFERENCE.
```

**Placement:** Mid-card area, paired with the 3-panel icon sequence.

**3-Panel Icon Sequence (IKEA-flat style):**

| Panel | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open palm with X over it (can't slap) |
| 2 | Hand slapping sticker onto someone's forehead/back (sticker application in progress) |
| 3 | Person standing with sticker on them, looking stunned. Checkmark. |

**Design note:** The icons turn the copy into an instructional diagram. Panel 1 shows the prohibition. Panel 2 shows the approved alternative. Panel 3 shows the result — legal, technically. The three panels map to the three sentences.

### Element 7: Accent Tagline

```
STICKS AND STONES MAY BREAK YOUR BONES. THESE STICK AND STAY.
```

**Placement:** Secondary tagline position, below feature area or above bottom edge.

**Design note:** Subverts the childhood saying. "Sticks and stones may break your bones but words will never hurt me" — these aren't words. They're adhesive. The childhood defense doesn't apply.

### Element 8: Badge

```
LEAST RETURNED PRODUCT IN THIS BRIEFCASE.
THEY DON'T COME OFF.
```

**Placement:** Corner badge element — ribbon/seal format similar to Cubicle Shank's "Good Shanking Seal of Approval" or Lifer Ink's "Dermatologist Recommended."

**Design note:** Self-awarded accolade. The first line sounds like a customer satisfaction metric. The second line reveals the reason is a product feature, not satisfaction — you can't return what you can't remove. The badge should be visually prominent and styled as an award/certification.

### Element 9: Age Recommendation

```
Suitable for Anyone With Enemies.
```

**Placement:** Bottom edge of card front.

## SURFACE 9: TRAY CARD BACK

**Job:** Sticker showcase with mob-style nicknames. Plus disclaimers and fine print. The sticker grid with nicknames is the centerpiece — the comedy lines fill around it.

### Element 1: Sticker Grid with Nicknames

**Description:** All 8 sticker designs displayed with their sticker text and a mob-style nickname for each. The nickname sits below or beside each sticker preview.

| # | Image | Sticker Text | Nickname |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dog silhouette lifting leg | MARKED. | Old Faithful |
| 2 | Three diagonal claw marks | PET FRIENDLY. | Teddy Soft Paws |
| 3 | Squirrel on pile of office supplies | WINTER'S COMING. | Nuts McGee |
| 4 | Skunk with raised tail | FINAL WARNING | Breezy |
| 5 | Cat pushing object off desk | RESTRUCTURING. | Butterfingers |
| 6 | Skull with pen shank | PREVIOUS OWNER. | The Temp |
| 7 | Three coffins (casket, filing cabinet, Viking boat) | HELP YOURSELF. OPTIONS AVAILABLE. | The Travel Agent |
| 8 | Four-panel IKEA pictogram | FYI. FOR REFERENCE. | 7 Points |

**Placement:** Main area of the back, taking up the majority of the surface.

**Design note:** The nicknames create a gap between what you'd expect from the name and what the sticker actually shows. "Teddy Soft Paws" next to bear claw marks. "Breezy" next to a skunk about to gas the room. "The Temp" next to a skull with a pen shank in it. The nicknames don't need a header or explanation — they sit with the sticker images and the reader figures out the joke through the contrast. The mob-name energy is communicated through the naming convention itself (definite articles, single-word aliases, first-name-last-name combos).

### Element 2: Duct Tape Disclaimer

```
IN A PINCH AND OUT OF DUCT TAPE? APPROVED FOR FACIAL USE.
MOUTH ONLY. LEAVE THE NOSE. — HR
```

**Placement:** Below or adjacent to the sticker grid. Disclaimer zone.

**Icon:** IKEA-flat face in profile with sticker over mouth. Small checkmark next to mouth. Small X next to nose. Instructional safety diagram showing correct vs. incorrect facial application zones.

**Design note:** Duct tape is the real-world joke about shutting someone up. The stickers are positioned as the office-appropriate alternative. "In a pinch" frames it as a contingency — a practical tip buried in the product instructions. The HR attribution implies this scenario came up enough to warrant a formal departmental policy.

### Element 3: Evidence Line

```
Cited in 3 workplace disputes and 1 divorce. The divorce was a referral.
```

**Placement:** Fine print area, bottom of card.

**Design note:** The numbers are specific — someone tracked this data. "The divorce was a referral" is the kicker — it implies someone used the stickers at work, caused a dispute, and then recommended them to someone who used them at home with marital consequences. The product didn't just cause a divorce — it was word-of-mouth marketed into one.

### Element 4: Best By

```
BEST BY: IMMEDIATELY. BEFORE THEY SIT DOWN.
```

**Placement:** Fine print area, near evidence line.

**Design note:** Real packaging "best by" format. The urgency implies the stickers work best as a preemptive strike — mark the territory before someone else claims the seat. "Before they sit down" is specific enough to create a visual.

## DESIGN STYLE REFERENCE

- **Box background:** Bold yellow — caution tape / warning sign / construction zone energy
- **Box type:** Black throughout, bold condensed sans-serif for headlines
- **Box accent:** Red divider line on front face
- **Box icons:** Black IKEA-flat line art style (consistent across Long Side A, Long Side B, back face product description icons)
- **Tray card background:** Dark/black (consistent with Cubicle Shank and Lifer Ink cards)
- **Tray card type:** White text on dark background, with yellow accents matching the box
- **Tray card badge:** Ribbon/seal format, visually prominent
- **Tray card icons:** Same IKEA-flat style as box surfaces for visual consistency
