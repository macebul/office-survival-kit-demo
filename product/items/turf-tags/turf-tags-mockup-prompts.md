## PROMPT NOTES

- These prompts are for Midjourney v6.1
- AI image generators struggle with exact text reproduction — the generated images will approximate the text layout and feel but individual words may be garbled or misspelled. The purpose of these mockups is to validate the **layout, density, visual hierarchy, and overall feel** — not to produce print-ready copy.
- The Turf Tags box uses a **bold yellow background with black type** — visually distinct from the dark/black backgrounds of Cubicle Shank and Lifer Ink. This is a deliberate contrast within the kit.
- The tray card uses a **dark/black background with yellow and white accents** — maintaining consistency with the other tray cards while incorporating the yellow color from the box.
- The `--s 250` flag sets stylization. Adjust up for more artistic interpretation, down for more literal rendering.
- To remove the 3x3 grid prefix for single-image generation, delete "Show me a 3x3 grid of" from the start of any prompt.
