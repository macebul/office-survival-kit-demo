# Cubicle Shank — Copy Plan

**Created:** October 26, 2026 · **Approved by owner:** October 26, 2026
**Scope:** Copy only. Layout, visuals, packaging format and the other items stay paused until this card's copy is locked.
**The approach:** a small card with fixed slots, filled through short rounds. Johnny's actual words decide everything.

---

## Ground rules (locked 2026-10-26)

1. **Voice:** Witty Yeti's warm, slightly naughty friend. No Brad Sterling or Elevate Co. on this card.
2. **Copy is written per slot, not per box surface.** Slots move to whatever surface the final format has, so the blister-vs-flap decision can't derail the copy.
3. **Lines Johnny writes are locked as written.** We offer alternatives alongside his line, never instead of it.
4. **Only Johnny's words change direction.** No mid-round restructuring based on our own new ideas.
5. **Stop rule:** a slot that fails 3 Johnny rounds is cut. Less copy is fine; weak copy is not.
6. **Johnny first sees something after one internal round** (Steps 2–4), as one tight batch (owner decision 2026-10-26).
7. **Card size: about 12 slots.** Everything else on today's card is cut or benched (owner decision 2026-10-26).
