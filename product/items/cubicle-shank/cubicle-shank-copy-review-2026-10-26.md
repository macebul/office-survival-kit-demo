## Overall

- **Card front: C.** The two best lines on the front were written by Johnny, and neither is on it. The brand block needs design notes to explain itself.
- **Card back: C+.** The best line on the whole card is there, but buried third. Around it sit a 28-cell chart, two coroner reviews and a nursing-home ad.
- **Hidden under the pen: A.** "Eventually." is approved.
- **Across the whole card:**
  - About 60 jokes in total, counting chart cells. Johnny would need about 10 minutes to read it all, which may explain the silence.
  - About 10 of the 25 elements are about legal fallout: trial, jury (twice), HR (3 times), Legal, coroner, medical examiner, character witness.
  - 4 elements depend on puns.
  - Kevin appears twice (the leak-proof line and a chart cell). The rules allow 0–1 per item.
  - Five recurring characters on one pen card: Kevin, Carl, Doug, Edith, the intern.
