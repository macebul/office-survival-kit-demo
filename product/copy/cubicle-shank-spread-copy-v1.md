## BEFORE / AFTER TRANSFORMATION

### Setup Line (above photos)

> REAL RESULTS. REAL KYLE.

### Visual Concept

**BEFORE photo:** Kyle at his desk — hunched, pathetic, someone reaching over him to grab his stuff, "INTERN" sticky note on his monitor, chewed-up Bic pen on the desk. Body language says "I accept this." Action shot, not a posed portrait.

**AFTER photo:** Same desk, but Kyle's leaned back, Cubicle Shank resting prominently on the desk. Empty cubicles around him. Someone left flowers. His lunch is untouched. Same intern badge. The environment changed around him. Action energy, not static.

### Result Copy (below photos)

> Same intern. Same desk. Within two weeks, Kyle's personal space expanded by 300%. His meetings got shorter. His reviews got better. His handshake got firmer.* Someone held the elevator. The pen didn't change Kyle's title. It changed everything else.
