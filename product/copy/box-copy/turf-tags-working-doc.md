### FRONT

**[BRAND LINE]** ELEVATE CO. — Territorial Compliance Division

**[PRODUCT NAME]** TURF TAGS™

**[MAIN COPY]** MARK YOUR TERRITORY

**[SUBTITLE]** Workspace Personalization Labels

**[TAGLINE]** *Because Asking Nicely Didn't Work.*

**[SPEC LINE]** Contains: 12 Adhesive Property Markers / For Surface Application Only*

**[FINE PRINT]** *Anything that holds still long enough.

**[VISUAL]** Silhouette of a dog peeing on an office chair

**Assessment:** Very strong front. "Territorial Compliance Division" is peak Brad institutional subdivision naming — matches "Dermal Achievement Division" quality. The dog-peeing-on-office-chair visual is doing enormous comedy work and is the kind of visual gag that WY products nail (see Child Chucker box art). "MARK YOUR TERRITORY" is a double meaning that lands instantly. "Because Asking Nicely Didn't Work" is a solid tagline — it's got personality and warmth, reads like someone talking to you, and the tone is closer to the WY cheerful-conspiratorial voice than much of the other v01 copy. The fine print ("Anything that holds still long enough") is a good escalation — takes "surface application" literally and implies you'll stick these on people. The whole front reads as a real product that knows exactly what it is. This is one of the stronger fronts across the item boxes.

**One concern:** "Workspace Personalization Labels" as a subtitle feels slightly redundant since it's Brad's official rebrand name that should probably be more prominent or integrated differently. Minor layout note, not a copy issue.

**Assessment:** Strong back panel. The "Territorial Assessment Report" form format is perfect Brad Mode 1 — institutional format played completely straight. "Form WPL-7" is a nice Brad touch (he loves form numbers). "WORKSPACE STATUS: ☑ VULNERABLE" and "THREAT LEVEL: ELEVATED" are both great format comedy — they look like real assessment forms with absurd content. The escalating percentages (75% → 100% → 0%) are a clean rule-of-three that lands on the right word: "face." The punchline is concrete and specific.

"Chief Territorial Officer™" is a strong Brad title (matches "Chief Dermal Achievement Officer"). The ™ on the title is perfect Brad Mode 2 — he trademarked his own made-up title.

Brad's quote — "I'm not territorial. I just know what's mine" — is one of the better Brad quotes across the item boxes. It's short (11 words), it's Brad Mode 2 (completely sincere, zero self-awareness), and it reveals character. Brad genuinely doesn't see the contradiction. This is better than the Lifer Ink Brad quote.

### LONG SIDE A (application icons)

**[ICONS]** PEEL — STICK — HOLD COURT — ACCEPT PARADE — EXIT

**[VISUALS]** Five icons: (1) hand peeling sticker, (2) hand placing sticker on surface, (3) person sitting on throne/chair like royalty, (4) person standing through car sunroof with confetti/ticker tape, (5) helicopter extraction

**Assessment:** This is the best application-instruction surface across ALL item boxes. The Lifer Ink version (CUT / WET / 30 SEC / DISPLAY) was good — this one is better. The escalation from mundane (PEEL, STICK) to absurd (HOLD COURT, ACCEPT PARADE, EXIT) is a perfect five-beat escalation pattern. The icons do the heavy lifting — the helicopter exit visual is laugh-out-loud funny and doesn't need a single word of explanation. "HOLD COURT" is a great word choice — it's both the literal thing (holding court = receiving subjects) and the prison-world resonance (holding court = running your territory). "ACCEPT PARADE" is perfectly absurd. "EXIT" with a helicopter is the punchline delivered visually.

This surface is genuinely at or above WY quality. No changes needed.

### LONG SIDE B (warning)

**[VISUAL]** Icon of a large person and a small person (the small person pointing/confronting the big person)

**[TEXT]** NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR DISPUTES OR CONFRONTATIONS WITH ANYONE BIGGER THAN YOU.

**Assessment:** Strong. The visual of a small person confronting a large person sets up the joke before you read the text. "Not responsible for disputes or confrontations" is perfect disclaimer format. "WITH ANYONE BIGGER THAN YOU" is a concrete, vivid punchline that lands on the last word. It implies you WILL get into confrontations — the company just can't help you with the ones you'll lose. The humor is in what it concedes: it's not saying "don't confront people," it's saying "we're not covering the ones where you're outmatched." This is WY-quality — it could sit on the Shart Kit box or the Child Chucker box without looking out of place.

No changes needed.

### SHORT SIDE A

**[TEXT]** SHARING IS ~~CARING~~ OVER.

*("CARING" is crossed out with strikethrough, making it read "SHARING IS OVER")*

**Assessment:** Perfect. Three words visible, two words implied, maximum impact. The strikethrough is a visual gag that works instantly — everyone recognizes "sharing is caring" and the violent editorial correction tells you everything about the product's worldview. This is a meme-ready surface. It's the kind of thing people photograph and share.

The technique is strong: taking a known phrase and defacing it is exactly what WY products do well (see "NOT FOR RESUSCITATION" type gags on other WY products). No changes needed.

### SHORT SIDE B

**[VISUAL]** Diamond warning sign with pedestrian icon (standard "No Trespassing" sign format)

**[TEXT]** NO TRESPASSING

**Assessment:** Perfect. Two words. Uses a universally recognized sign format. The comedy is context-dependent — a "No Trespassing" sign on a sticker box for your desk is both completely appropriate (territory marking) and completely absurd (it's an office, not a ranch). The sign format does the work. No changes needed.

### TUCK FLAP (visible when box is opened)

**[TEXT]** START SMALL. THEN DON'T.

**Assessment:** Very strong. Four words. Two sentences. The setup is a reasonable instruction ("start small" — put a sticker on your mug first). The punchline is the permission to escalate. "Then don't" is the brand being conspiratorial — it's saying "we both know you're going to go bigger." This is close to the WY voice: warm, encouraging, slightly irresponsible. It could be a touch more vivid/specific, but at four words on a tuck flap, it's doing its job perfectly.

No changes needed.

## SECTION 4: OVERALL ASSESSMENT

**This is the strongest item box in the v01 batch.**

Every surface is working. The front has a killer visual (the dog), a strong tagline with personality, and good fine print. The back has one of the best Brad quotes across all items. The application icons are the single funniest instruction surface in the entire kit. Both long sides deliver. Both short sides deliver. The tuck flap delivers.

Compared to WY reference products:
- "Because Asking Nicely Didn't Work" has the warmth and conspiratorial tone of Shart Kit's "Give the gift of preparedness to anyone who's ever gambled on a fart and lost"
- "NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR DISPUTES OR CONFRONTATIONS WITH ANYONE BIGGER THAN YOU" reads like it could sit on Child Chucker packaging
- "SHARING IS ~~CARING~~ OVER" has the phrase-defacing energy of Dehydrated Water's "Gluten-free, cruelty-free, hydration-free"
- The application icons (PEEL → STICK → HOLD COURT → ACCEPT PARADE → EXIT) are at or above the quality of any step-by-step on existing WY products

**If the boss's blanket "not funny enough" rejection was driven by weaker items in the batch (Cubicle Shank v01, possibly the card decks), Turf Tags may have been collateral damage.** This box reads like a finished, shippable WY product.

**Only fix needed:** Reconcile "12 Adhesive Property Markers" with actual sticker count (6-10 per spec). Same issue as Lifer Ink's "12 Commendations."

## SECTION 7: KEY LEARNINGS

1. **Turf Tags is the strongest v01 item box.** Every surface is working. The visual comedy (dog, helicopter, sign formats) does enormous lifting. The copy is tight and fits the physical surfaces.

2. **The dog visual is carrying the front.** The silhouette of a dog peeing on an office chair is immediately funny before you read a word. This is WY-grade visual comedy.

3. **The application icons are the single best comedy surface in the kit.** PEEL → STICK → HOLD COURT → ACCEPT PARADE → EXIT (helicopter) is a five-beat escalation that doesn't need any copy improvement.

4. **Brad's quote on this item is better than on Lifer Ink.** "I'm not territorial. I just know what's mine" reveals character, is short, and is genuinely funny in a Brad Mode 2 way.

5. **The same "12 count" issue exists here.** The v01 says "12 Adhesive Property Markers" but the spec says 6-10 stickers. Either the count changes or the sticker count increases.

6. **If anything on this box was part of the rejection, it was likely the back panel format.** The "Territorial Assessment Report" is strong institutional comedy but it's the most "format-heavy" surface — if the boss wanted more personality/warmth/WY voice, this is the one surface where the format might feel too dry. But even then, the percentages joke and Brad's quote rescue it.

7. **This box should be sent to the boss as-is (with the count fix) and specific feedback requested.** There's no obvious weak surface to improve proactively.
