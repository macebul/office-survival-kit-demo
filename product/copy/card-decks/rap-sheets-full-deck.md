# Rap Sheets — Complete Deck
## Interpersonal Feedback Delivery System™

**Format:**
- **Front top:** MOST LIKELY TO BE CHARGED WITH:
- **Front middle:** The crime (with comedy beats)
- **Front bottom:** ☐ Witnessed  ☐ Suspected  ☐ Inevitable
- **Back:** SENTENCE (absurd institutional punishment)

**Previous format:** Front = Charge + Brad's response. Then Charge + Sentence. Updated July 6, 2026 to "Most Likely To Be Charged With" superlative format — more playful, easier to hand out (nomination vs. accusation), raises the absurdity ceiling since the crime doesn't need to have actually happened. Checkboxes at bottom make it interactive.
