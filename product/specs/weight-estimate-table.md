## Weight Calculation Methodology

**Paperboard surface area approach:** For folded boxes, total surface area of all panels (including flaps/tucks) is calculated, then multiplied by the weight-per-square-inch for that caliper. For 24pt SBS paperboard, ~0.044 oz/sq in. For 18pt, ~0.033 oz/sq in. For 14-16pt card stock, ~0.028 oz/sq in.

## Key Observations

1. **The blister card backings are the heaviest component group** at ~28.7 oz (30% of total weight). Seven L-shaped 18pt paperboard cards at ~124.5 sq in each add up. This is the trade-off for having 1,743 sq in of comedy surface.

2. **The briefcase shell is the heaviest single component** at ~22 oz (23% of total weight). This is driven by the 24pt paperboard multi-layer rigid construction at a large 14x11.5x5.0" footprint. Typical for rigid setup boxes at this size.

3. **The outer Amazon box** at ~18 oz (19% of total weight) is the second heaviest single component. Corrugated at this size is unavoidably heavy.

4. **The mug remains the heaviest item** at ~11 oz. Switching to 8oz stainless would save roughly 3-4 oz.

5. **Packaging outweighs contents.** Structural packaging + blister system totals ~76 oz vs. ~18 oz for actual kit items. This ~80/20 packaging-to-content ratio is higher than the old tray system because the 7 large blister cards add significant paperboard weight. The trade-off is dramatically more comedy surface area.

6. **FBA weight compliance is not a concern.** At under 6 lbs total, the kit sits at roughly 30% of the 20 lb Large Standard Size limit. Even if every estimate here is 20% light, the kit would still be under 7.2 lbs.

7. **Shipping cost sensitivity:** At ~5.94 lbs actual weight in a 15.5x13x6.5" box, FBA fulfillment fees will be driven by dimensional weight, not actual weight. The box volume (1,310.75 cubic inches) converts to ~9.4 lbs dimensional weight at the standard 139 divisor — dimensional weight exceeds actual weight and will be the cost driver.

## Disclaimers

- All weights are estimates based on standard material densities and typical product weights. Actual weights will vary based on exact material specs from sourcing.
- Paperboard weights vary significantly by manufacturer, coating, and lamination. Request actual material weight sheets from the box supplier.
- The mug weight is the highest-variance item — confirm with supplier samples.
- Card deck weights depend on final card count (10 vs. 15 vs. 20 per deck). Estimates above use 15 cards.
- PETG blister shell weights are rough estimates — actual weight depends on wall thickness and forming depth. Request samples from thermoform supplier.
