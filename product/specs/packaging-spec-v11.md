## 2. Concept

**Brad Sterling** (B.S., Chief Vision Officer & Founder of **Elevate Co.**) is the sole voice of the entire product. Every surface — outer box, briefcase, blister packs, card boxes, mug box — is Brad's creation. He has packaged each item as a retail product under the Elevate Co. brand, complete with his over-the-top marketing copy and trademarked rebranded names.

The blister packs are designed to look like authentic retail products. Someone should pick up the pen blister pack and think it's a real product for 2-3 seconds before they start reading the copy and realize it's comedy. The packaging dimensions should feel natural and proportional for each item type.

**Design principle:** Format stays buttoned-up, content gets unleashed. Official labels, FDA formatting, corporate branding stay polished. The words inside go for laughs on every line.

## 4. Interior Layout — Overview

The interior is a single open space with no trays, no dividers, and no compartments. Items are individually packaged and stacked directly inside the briefcase.

**Layout:**
- 7 L-shaped blister pack cards stacked flat (card backing parallel to briefcase base)
- 1 mug box sitting beside the blister stack in the top-left corner
- Blister bubbles face up (toward the lid)
- Card backings face down (toward the base)

**Interior is fully available:** 13.5" × 11.0" × 4.5" — no zones, no fixed structures.

### Dimensions

| Measurement | Value | Notes |
|-------------|-------|-------|
| Overall width | 13.25" | 13.5" interior − 0.125" clearance each side |
| Overall height | 10.75" | 11.0" interior − 0.125" clearance each side |
| Notch width | 4.75" | Mug box (5.0") − 0.25" overlap buffer |
| Notch height | 3.75" | Mug box (4.0") − 0.25" overlap buffer |
| Zone A | 13.25" × 7.00" | Full width, below the mug box line |
| Zone B | 8.50" × 3.75" | To the right of the mug box |
| Total card area | 124.5 sq in | (13.25 × 7.00) + (8.50 × 3.75) |
| Total printable area | 249.0 sq in | Front + back per card |
| Hanging hole | 0.25" diameter | Centered on top edge, 0.375" from edge |

### Construction

- Material: 18pt paperboard (0.025" thick), printed both sides
- Die cut: L-shape with rounded corners (0.125" radius) and hanging hole
- All 7 cards use the same die — only the print differs
- Blister: Clear PETG thermoformed plastic, glued to the front of the card backing

### Why L-Shaped

The notch in the top-left corner is sized to clear the mug box, which sits beside the blister stack in the same position across all layers. This maximizes card area while allowing the mug box to coexist at full briefcase depth.

### Both Sides Are Comedy Surfaces

- **Front:** Product branding, item visible through blister, comedy copy filling every available inch around the blister
- **Back:** Deep comedy copy — fake FDA warnings, usage instructions, testimonials, QC stamps, fine print, comparison charts. Every square inch should have intentional comedy or intentional design space. No dead zones.

The recipient lifts each pack, laughs at the front, flips it over, laughs at the back, then opens the blister to get the item — three layers of engagement per pack.

## 7. Evidence Tag System

Each of the 7 blister packs has an evidence tag attached to the back of the card by a short ribbon. The tags serve two purposes: (1) extraction — the recipient grabs the tag to pull each blister card out of the tightly fitted briefcase, and (2) comedy — each tag is a unique micro comedy surface styled as a police evidence property tag.

### Evidence Tag Spec

| Attribute | Spec |
|-----------|------|
| Tag dimensions | ~2.0" × 1.25" |
| Tag material | Heavy card stock (14-16pt), manila/cream colored |
| Tag corners | Slightly rounded |
| Tag print | Full color, both sides |
| Reinforcement hole | Small reinforced hole punched in the top center of the tag for ribbon threading |
| Quantity | 7 (one per blister pack) |

### Tag Design

Each tag is styled as a police evidence property tag:

- **Front:** Red "EVIDENCE" header in distressed uppercase stencil text, thin red border, printed fields for Case No., Item name (Brad's rebranded name for that item), Date Seized, Logged By (rotating between Gary and other recurring characters), and small "CHAIN OF CUSTODY — DO NOT REMOVE" fine print at the bottom
- **Back:** Additional comedy copy — could include evidence handling instructions, witness statements, or item-specific jokes. This is a unique comedy surface per tag (7 unique backs × ~2.5 sq in = ~17.5 sq in additional comedy surface).

Each tag has unique copy. The 7 case numbers, item names, dates, and comedy copy are all different.

### Ribbon Spec

| Attribute | Spec |
|-----------|------|
| Material | Natural tan cotton twill ribbon |
| Width | ~0.5" (half inch) |
| Total length | ~2.5" (1.0" adhered to card back + 1.5" free hanging to tag) |
| Color | Natural tan / manila — matches the tag color |
| Attachment to tag | Threaded through the reinforced hole in the tag and knotted |
| Attachment to card | Glued flat to the back (underside) of the blister card with a small rectangular adhesive patch (~1.0" × 0.5"), centered on the bottom edge of the card, approximately 0.5" up from the bottom card edge |

### Positioning in Briefcase

- The ribbon and tag hang from the bottom center edge of each blister card
- When the cards are stacked in the briefcase, the tags and ribbons rest in the narrow gap between the card edges and the front interior wall (clasp edge)
- When the briefcase is opened and viewed from above, the top card's evidence tag is visible resting against the front interior wall — the cream/manila tag pops against the dark charcoal interior
- The 7 tags stack on top of each other in the gap, with the top card's tag on top (most visible)
- The tags are the only light-colored elements visible against the dark interior, naturally drawing the eye and inviting the recipient to grab and pull

### Extraction Function

The cards fit snugly in the briefcase (0.125" clearance per side). The evidence tag is how the recipient extracts each card:

1. Open briefcase
2. See the evidence tag resting against the front wall
3. Grab the tag
4. Pull upward — the ribbon lifts the card out of the briefcase
5. Examine both sides of the blister card, open the blister to get the item
6. The next card's evidence tag is now visible — repeat

### Assembly

Each tag+ribbon assembly is glued to the back of each blister card as a manual assembly step. The tag and free ribbon portion are folded flat against the card back during packing, then unfold naturally when the card is placed in the briefcase and the ribbon hangs into the gap at the front edge.

### Comedy Notes

- The evidence tag concept reinforces the kit's world: the briefcase is confiscated evidence, each item is tagged
- "CHAIN OF CUSTODY — DO NOT REMOVE" on every tag is both funny and functional (it tells the recipient the tag is supposed to be there)
- "Logged By: Gary" should appear on at least 2-3 of the 7 tags (Gary is the recurring background character who appears on stamps/badges/inspection marks — see character reference)
- The tags are a detail people will notice and photograph for social media — small, discoverable, high shareability

**Status: FITS.** 0.40" clearance is ideal — items won't rattle but aren't jammed. The mug box has slightly more clearance (0.50") because it's shorter than the blister stack.

### Blister Bubble Placement Guide (Stack Stability)

All 7 cards stack in the same orientation (notch must always clear the mug box — rotation is not possible). To keep the stack flat and stable, vary the blister bubble position across layers so bumps from one layer don't stack on bumps from the layer below.

| Layer | Item | Recommended Blister Placement |
|-------|------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | Contraband (stapler shape) | Center-right of Zone A or spanning Zone A/B junction |
| 2 | Cell Talk (card deck box) | Center of Zone A |
| 3 | Rap Sheets (card deck box) | Left side of Zone A |
| 4 | Turf Tags (sticker sheet, 6.5" × 4.5") | Center of Zone A — dome footprint ~6.7" × 4.7"; 0.15" deep, acts as leveling layer |
| 5 | Sweet Nothings (card deck box) | Right side of Zone A |
| 6 | Lifer Ink (tattoo sheet, 6.5" × 4.5") | Center of Zone A — dome footprint ~6.7" × 4.7" (identical to sticker sheet); 0.15" deep, acts as leveling layer |
| 7 | Cubicle Shank (pen) | Horizontal across Zone A — naturally offset from square bubbles below |

These are recommendations, not rigid constraints. The designer has freedom — just avoid stacking three 0.80" bubbles in the exact same spot across Layers 2, 3, and 5.

## 17. Assembly Sequence

1. Place mug box in top-left corner of briefcase interior (resting on briefcase floor)
2. Stack blister packs flat, bottom to top:
   - Layer 1: Contraband (gag)
   - Layer 2: Cell Talk
   - Layer 3: Rap Sheets
   - Layer 4: Turf Tags
   - Layer 5: Sweet Nothings
   - Layer 6: Lifer Ink
   - Layer 7: Cubicle Shank (on top)
3. Ensure lid pocket cards are inserted (check-in card + map)
4. Close lid (magnets engage)
5. Place briefcase in outer Amazon box with padding
6. Close and seal outer box

2a. Before stacking, attach evidence tag+ribbon assemblies to the back of each blister card (glued flat — see Section 7)

3a. Place foam compression pad on top of the blister stack (on top of Layer 7: Cubicle Shank)

**Assembly is straightforward** — evidence tag attachment is a manual pre-assembly step, then sequential stacking with no complex nesting or alignment required.

**Extraction:** Each blister card has an evidence tag (manila card stock on cotton twill ribbon) attached to its back. The tags rest against the front interior wall when cards are stacked. The recipient grabs the visible tag, pulls upward, and the ribbon lifts the card out. The 1.5" extraction notch in the front wall provides supplementary fingertip access. See Section 7 for full evidence tag spec.

## 24. Notes for the Designer

1. **Blister card die cut:** One L-shaped die serves all 7 cards. Only the print changes per item.
2. **Hanging hole:** 0.25" diameter, centered on the top edge, 0.375" from the edge. Present on all 7 cards for retail authenticity.
3. **Card deck boxes:** All 3 are the same dimensions. Only the print differs.
4. **Mug box window:** Die-cut window on one face. Shape/size is a design decision (circular or rectangular).
5. **Blister bubble placement:** Vary positions across layers to prevent stacking instability (see Section 8).
6. **The Contraband pack** has an empty stapler-shaped blister cavity. The card copy should read like an evidence tag or confiscation notice.
7. **Both sides are comedy surfaces.** Front: product branding + blister. Back: deep comedy copy. Every square inch should have intentional comedy or intentional design space.
8. **This is a comedy product.** The blister packs should look like real retail products for 2-3 seconds before the comedy lands. Match proportions of real retail packaging in each category.
