# Briefcase Exterior — Image Prompts

**Last Updated:** April 2026
**Status:** v2
**Purpose:** Image generation prompts for each briefcase exterior surface. Each prompt generates one flat texture map that maps directly onto the corresponding face of the 3D model. Use these as starting points — refine based on output.
**Reference:** All copy from exterior-copy-v4.md. All visual treatments from exterior-spec-v3.md. All dimensions from dimensions-spec-v10.md.

**Dimensions:** Briefcase is 14" L × 11.5" W × 5.0" D.

**Critical aesthetic note:** The briefcase exterior is 100% Brad Sterling / Elevate Co. territory. The aesthetic is institutional, worn, slightly scuffed matte black paperboard — but it's Brad's version of "rugged authenticity." Rubber stamps with imperfect ink. Aged cream/kraft paper labels with torn edges. Gray duct tape. Monospace typewriter fonts. Evidence tags. The aesthetic is: government-issued field kit assembled under difficult conditions. Somewhere between "serious" and "scrappy." It looks intentional and considered — not sloppy — but clearly improvised and homemade. The joke is that it looks like it means business. The details reveal it's contraband. Brad Sterling and Elevate Co. are the sole voice on all exterior surfaces.

**Rendering style (ALL prompts):** Photorealistic product photography, shot perfectly straight-on with even studio lighting. Real matte black paperboard material with visible fiber texture. Labels, stickers, and stamps look like real physical objects adhered to a real surface — visible edge thickness on stickers, ink absorption on stamps, paper grain on labels. The surface should look like you could reach out and touch it. NOT flat graphic design, NOT vector art, NOT illustration. These are texture maps for a 3D model that needs to look like a real physical product.

**Color palette:** Matte black (box surface), aged cream/kraft yellow (labels), red (rubber stamps — faded, not bright), gray (duct tape handle), light gray/silver (printed clasp graphics), white (QC sticker, small labels). NO gold. NO purple. NO corporate colors.

**Applied element finish:** All stickers, labels, and stamps have a subtle satin finish so they read as separate objects sitting ON the matte surface. Nothing glossy.

## Usage Notes

- All prompts designed for use with Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, or similar
- All prompts render as PHOTOREALISTIC product photography — real materials, real textures, real lighting. NOT flat graphic design, NOT vector, NOT illustration.
- Each artboard generates one flat texture map for one face of the 3D model
- Aspect ratios per surface:
  - Artboards 1 & 4 (Lid/Bottom, 14" × 11.5"): **--ar 5:4** (or --ar 14:11.5)
  - Artboards 2 & 3 (Front/Back, 14" × 5"): **--ar 3:1** (or --ar 14:5)
  - Artboards 5 & 6 (Edges, 11.5" × 5"): **--ar 23:10** (or --ar 11.5:5)
  - Artboard 7 belly band: **TBD / under review**
- For 3D model texturing, generate at the highest resolution available and upscale before applying
- The Nutrition Facts panel (Artboard 3) will likely require multiple attempts — FDA label formatting is notoriously difficult for AI image generators. Consider generating the Nutrition Facts label as a standalone image and compositing it into the full back face panel
- The MISSING poster (Artboard 6) may need the stapler sketch generated separately and composited for best results
- The PLU produce sticker (Artboard 4) may need standalone generation at high res and compositing into the bottom surface
