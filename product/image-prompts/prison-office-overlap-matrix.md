# Prison / Office Overlap Matrix — Image Concept Generator

**Version:** 1.0
**Created:** May 18, 2026
**Purpose:** Reference document for generating booklet image concepts by finding comedic collisions between prison tropes and office life. Every image should exploit one of these overlaps.

## PRISON

### Daily Life
- Yard time / exercise hour
- Mess hall meals — terrible food, trays, long tables
- Head counts / roll call
- Lights out / lockdown
- Shower block
- Commissary purchases
- Cell inspections
- Laundry duty

### Power Dynamics
- Guards watching from elevated positions
- Solitary confinement as punishment
- Good behavior earning privileges
- Snitches / informants
- Gang territories and alliances
- The shot caller who runs things from inside
- New fish / fresh meat arrival
- Establishing dominance on day one

### Escape & Resistance
- Tunnel digging
- Filing through bars
- Hiding contraband in cells
- Smuggling things in/out
- Bribing guards
- The wall / the fence / razor wire
- Escape plans drawn on hidden maps
- Counting days with tally marks on the wall

### Rituals & Culture
- Trading cigarettes / ramen as currency
- Tattoos marking time served or rank
- Making shanks from everyday objects
- Toilet wine / pruno
- Reading in your cell
- Working out obsessively
- Writing letters home
- Visitation day — sitting across from someone through glass
- Parole hearings
- Jumpsuit / uniform

### Events
- Riots
- Lockdowns after incidents
- New warden arrives — everything changes
- Contraband sweep / cell toss
- Someone gets released — everyone watches
- Transfer to another facility
- Conjugal visits

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

1. Pick an overlap from the matrix
2. Write the scene as a single, clear image — one or two characters, one action, one punchline
3. Match to existing booklet copy, or use the collision to inspire new copy
4. Apply the art direction brief (photorealistic, stock photo aesthetic, deadpan delivery)
5. Nobody in the frame acknowledges the absurdity — it's all completely normal to them
