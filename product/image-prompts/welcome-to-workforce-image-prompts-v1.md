## Image 1: Hero — Group Orientation Scene (pp. 4-5, left page)

**Purpose:** The hero image for the spread. University welcome brochure energy. This is the first photograph the reader sees in the editorial section of the magazine.

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape (hero image, spans 4 columns on left page)

**Art direction:** Bright, warm, aspirational — like a photo from a university's admissions brochure. A group of new hires being processed during an orientation session in a corporate office. The tone should feel genuinely welcoming and enthusiastic, but the details reveal the institutional reality. Professional editorial photography quality.

### Prompt

```
A bright warm editorial photograph of a corporate new-hire orientation session in a modern open-plan office, shot in the style of a university admissions brochure. A small group of six to eight adults of diverse genders and races stand in a line at a processing counter, each holding clipboards and branded welcome folders. A cheerful HR coordinator with a tablet is scanning a lanyard barcode for the person at the front of the line. Large banners on the wall behind them read motivational slogans. Everyone is smiling warmly at the camera except one person near the back who looks slightly bewildered. The new hires all wear identical lanyards with fresh ID badges that have obviously just been photographed — the badge photos are slightly awkward. The lighting is bright warm natural light from tall office windows, golden hour quality, the kind of lighting that makes everything feel aspirational. The composition is wide enough to show the full group and the branded orientation setup behind them. Shot at eye level with a wide-angle editorial lens, shallow depth of field on the background. The overall mood is aggressively welcoming — like a recruitment photo that's trying a little too hard --no cartoon, illustration, drawing, painting, sketch, anime, manga, chibi, stylized, flat color, cel shading, vector art, mid-century modern, geometric faces, angular faces, pointed noses, exaggerated proportions, caricature, waxy skin, plastic skin, airbrushed skin, unnaturally smooth skin, symmetrical face, perfect features, model-beautiful, glamour lighting, fashion photography, Instagram filter, color grading, teal and orange, oversaturated, HDR, lens flare, vignette, film grain for style, collage, composite, split screen, floating elements, text overlay, watermark, stock photo watermark, Getty Images watermark --ar 4:3 --s 250 --v 6.1

**Aspect ratio:** 1:1 square (small supporting image, 2 columns)

**Art direction:** Tight crop on a corporate ID badge clipped to a lanyard resting on someone's chest. The badge photo is slightly unflattering — taken from too close, with overhead fluorescent lighting — but the badge itself is professionally produced. ### Prompt

## Image 4: Page 7 — Hero Listing Photo (real estate listing)

**Purpose:** The hero listing photo for the cubicle real estate listing. This is the "money shot" — the cubicle photographed exactly like a luxury apartment listing on Zillow.

**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 landscape (hero listing photo, spans full page width)

**Art direction:** A single cubicle photographed with the warmth, angle, and staging of a luxury real estate listing. The cubicle has been deliberately "staged" — a small desk lamp with warm light, a neat potted plant, a clean desk with minimal items arranged perfectly, a motivational calendar. The photographer has used the same techniques you'd see in a Zillow listing: slightly wide angle, warm color temperature, standing in the "doorway" (the cubicle opening) shooting in. The result should make the viewer's brain register "real estate listing" before registering "cubicle."

### Prompt

```
A professional real estate listing photograph of a single corporate cubicle, shot from the opening of the cubicle looking in, using the exact same warm wide-angle technique used for Zillow luxury apartment photos. The cubicle has been deliberately staged like a property showing: a small warm-toned desk lamp is turned on casting golden light, a healthy potted plant sits on the corner of the desk, papers are neatly stacked, a clean coffee mug is placed at an inviting angle, and the office chair is positioned as if someone just stepped away. The cubicle walls are standard gray fabric panels that stop at about five feet. Beyond the cubicle walls, the fluorescent-lit office stretches into a soft blur. The photograph uses warm color temperature and slightly wide-angle lens to make the tiny space look as spacious and inviting as possible, exactly like real estate photography tricks.
