# Booklet Pages 10-15 — Photorealistic Image Prompts

**Last Updated:** May 19, 2026
**Status:** Draft v1 — all new prompts, no conversions
**Visual style:** Internal corporate communications photography. Flat overhead fluorescent lighting, beige walls, drop ceilings, gray carpet. Photos look like they were taken for the company intranet. Nobody reacts to absurdity.
**Generator:** Midjourney v6.1
**Booklet size:** A5 saddle-stitched, ~5.5" x 7" portrait. Images sized for spread placement.

**Brad's Visual DNA (when Brad appears):**
Purple satin shirt (always), unbuttoned to mid-chest minimum, oversized gold rope chain — comically large, think 80s rapper, impossible to miss, aviator sunglasses, gold signet pinky ring. Late 30s but dresses like a 22-year-old who watched Wolf of Wall Street once. Brad is NOT in every image — the booklet is about the reader's office world seen through Brad's lens, not a Brad character study.

## PAGES 10-11 — "The Ink" | PHASE 2: DIAGNOSIS (SPREAD)

### Copy Context

> New suit. New haircut. Nice watch.
> None of it proves anything.
> Where's the mark for the lunch that didn't make it?
> For being a flight risk?
> You earned your ink. You just haven't worn it yet.

### Comedy Beat

Dismisses surface-level status symbols. Names REAL survival moments (stolen lunch = crime scene, "flight risk" = dual meaning). "You earned your ink" reframes temporary tattoos as earned marks. Primes reader for Lifer Ink tattoos in Tray 1.

**Layer 2:** All surface polish, no substance. The reflection shows someone who looks ready but hasn't earned anything yet.

## NOTES

### Diverse Casting
Pages 10-11 and 12-13 feature non-Brad characters. Casting is diverse across the spread — no two adjacent vignettes should default to the same demographic. The prompts leave specific ethnicity flexible where it is not plot-critical, but generators should be guided toward variety across the full set.

### Brad Consistency
Brad appears in two prompts (12B and 14A). His visual DNA is identical in both — purple satin shirt, gold chain, aviators, pinky ring — but the camera treatment differs dramatically. In 12B, the camera is flat and corporate (Brad is just another person in the office). In 14A, the camera elevates him (Brad's self-image made real). This contrast IS the joke across the spread.

### Style Pivot from Earlier Pages
Pages 2-9 of this booklet use mid-century modern illustration via Nano Banana 2. Pages 10-15 are the first pages using photorealistic corporate photography via Midjourney v6.1. If the full booklet ultimately needs visual consistency, these prompts serve as the photorealistic direction reference — the earlier pages would need to be re-prompted to match, not the other way around. The photorealistic style was chosen for pages 10-15 because the comedy of "The Ink," "The Code," and "The Reveal" lands harder when the images look like real internal corporate communications photography rather than stylized illustration.
