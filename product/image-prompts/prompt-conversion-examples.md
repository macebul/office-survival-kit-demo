# Prompt Conversion Examples — Mid-Century to Photorealistic

**Purpose:** Demonstrates the old-to-new prompt translation for two key scenes. Use these as reference when rewriting all remaining prompts.

---

## Example 1: The Hazmat Response (Page 6-7, V2)

**Comedy beat:** A full biohazard containment team has been deployed to deal with a single ballpoint pen sitting on a conference room table. The pen is tiny and unremarkable. The response is apocalyptic.

### OLD PROMPT (Mid-Century Modern)

> Show me a 3x3 grid of [...] Three figures in yellow biohazard suits with full gas-mask face coverage [...] Yellow-and-black CAUTION tape crisscrossing the doorway in an X [...] Boxy bomb-squad robot with mechanical claw extending toward the pen [...] Small news helicopter visible through a window in the background [...] The pen alone on an otherwise empty office table in center foreground, normal scale, no special treatment [...] Cream/amber/charcoal color blocks, clean white background [...] stylized cartoon illustration, mid-century modern aesthetic, ALL characters have geometric angular faces with pointed triangular noses [...]

**What worked:** Clear scene hierarchy, the pen as uncontested focal point, the escalation from single object to institutional overreaction.

**What changes:** Everything visual. The scene stays the same. The comedy beat stays the same. The rendering becomes photorealistic.

### What Changed

| Element | Old | New |
|---------|-----|-----|
| **Style** | Mid-century illustration, flat color, geometric faces | Photorealistic photography, natural features |
| **Environment** | Color blocks + white background | Real conference room with drop ceiling, fluorescent lights, glass walls |
| **Characters** | Gas masks (faces hidden — worked around angular face requirement) | Gas masks with full hazmat suits rendered realistically |
| **Lighting** | Implied (no specific source) | Overhead fluorescent panels — institutional, flat, real |
| **Background jokes added** | None (mid-century version was simpler) | News helicopter, ACTIVE SCENE sign, forgotten coffee cup |
| **Scale gag** | Pen alone on empty table | Pen alone on full-sized conference table — the real-world proportions make the pen look even smaller |
| **Negatives** | Anti-red-nose, anti-illustration-artifacts | Anti-cartoon, anti-AI-artifacts, anti-stylization |

### Why This Scene Works Better in Photorealistic

The hazmat team reads as *actually real* — their suits look like actual CBRN equipment, their body language looks like trained emergency responders. When the camera pulls back and reveals they're converging on a Bic pen on a conference table, the viewer's brain has to reconcile "real emergency response" with "plastic pen." That reconciliation is the laugh. In cartoon form, everything was already abstracted, so the gap was smaller.

The visual quality gap between this image and the flat-lit hazmat scene on the adjacent page is Brad's self-image made visible.

## Key Translation Principles

When converting any mid-century prompt to photorealistic:

1. **Replace style anchors** — Swap the mid-century illustration anchor for the photorealistic anchor (or Brad cinematic variant)
2. **Remove geometric face DNA** — No more angular faces, triangular noses, defined jawlines. Describe people naturally.
3. **Remove named skin-tone anchors** — No more "pale peach beige" or "deep warm umber brown" as nose-color controls. Use natural descriptors ("light skin," "dark brown skin," "olive skin").
4. **Add real environment details** — Replace "color blocks floating behind the scene" with actual office furniture, ceilings, lighting fixtures, and surfaces.
5. **Add background jokes** — The photorealistic direction creates space for Layer 3 details that the flat mid-century style couldn't support. Use it.
6. **Ground expressions in reality** — Replace cartoon expression language ("wide terrified eyes," "cartoon silent scream") with real human expression descriptions ("the expression of someone who has been on hold for forty minutes," "completely unbothered").
7. **Swap negative prompts** — Replace anti-red-nose block with anti-cartoon/anti-AI-artifact block.
8. **Keep the comedy beat identical** — The joke doesn't change. Only the visual container changes.
