# Office Survival Kit — Document Index

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026 (fully rewritten; the June 24 version is in git history)
**Purpose:** The full file map. Every live document, with its last-changed date and its status. The status dashboard is [START-HERE.md](../../START-HERE.md); start there.

**Status labels**
- **CURRENT** — the live source of truth for its topic
- **STALE** — out of date, but nothing newer has replaced it yet; read with caution
- **REFERENCE** — background material, not product state
- **TOOL** — session prompts, scripts and templates, not product content
- **PAUSED** — current, but its area is on hold until the Shank copy is locked

**Dates** are each file's last-changed date in 2026 (MM-DD).

**Two packaging pivots haven't been carried into older docs:**
1. Trays and tuck-end boxes became an L-shaped **blister card** (June).
2. A cardboard **fold-over flap** is being explored to replace the plastic blister (07-06).

Anything dated before mid-June that describes trays, tuck-end boxes or the compartment cover is describing the old design.
