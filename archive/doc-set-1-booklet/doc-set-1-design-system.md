# Doc Set 1 — Design System

**Last Updated:** April 9, 2026
**Status:** Draft v1
**Format:** 16-page saddle-stitched booklet, ~5.5" x 7" portrait
**Style:** Startup brand book — clean, polished, varied formats united by consistent typography, color, and production quality
**Image Generation:** Midjourney via Nano Banana 2

---

## Typography

Two font families — one for authority, one for personality. Brad would choose fonts that look expensive.

### Primary: Sans-Serif (Headers, Labels, Brad's Voice)
**Recommendation: Montserrat or Gotham**
- Clean, geometric, modern
- Used for: page headers, phase labels, the loadout labels (NERVE AGENT, PERIMETER DEFENSE), Brad's name, section titles
- Weights: Bold for headers, Medium for labels, Light for secondary text
- Always uppercase for labels and phase markers

### Secondary: Serif (Quotes, Copy, Punchlines)
**Recommendation: Playfair Display or Freight Display**
- Elegant, editorial, high-contrast
- Used for: Brad's quotes, the main copy on each page, the closing "Nobody's trying to win"
- This is the font that makes the booklet feel like a real brand publication
- Mixed case, generous letter-spacing

### Accent: Monospace (Data, Fine Print, Technical Elements)
**Recommendation: Space Mono or IBM Plex Mono**
- Used for: the tactical diagram specs, the fine print on page 16, any "data" or "classified" elements
- Reinforces the institutional/military format subversion on the loadout pages

**Typography Hierarchy Example:**

```
PHASE 1: ASSESSMENT          ← Sans-serif, uppercase, gold, small
                             
They call it an office.      ← Serif, large, charcoal
I call it the block.         ← Serif, large, charcoal

BLOCK C - 00247              ← Monospace, small, charcoal
CELL 4B                      
STATUS: SERVING              
```

## Page Texture / Background

- **Interior pages:** Off-white/cream (#F5F0E8) with a very subtle paper texture — not heavy grain, just enough to feel warm and printed rather than digital. Think premium uncoated stock.
- **Cover (page 1) and back cover (page 16):** Matte black (#1A1A1A) with gold and white text. Gold foil catches light against the dark briefcase interior, creating contrast through material behavior (reflective foil on matte surfaces) rather than background color. Dyed-through black stock preferred; if printing black on white stock, use rich black (C60 M40 Y40 K100).
- **All interior pages:** Off-white/cream throughout. No dark interior pages — keeps COGS down and design simple.
- **No distressed/aged textures.** The OG Manual uses worn paper and coffee stains — that's the guerrilla aesthetic. Brad's booklet is CLEAN. New. Polished. The paper looks like it was printed yesterday because Brad would never ship anything that looked used.

---

## Layout Grid

Consistent margins and spacing across all pages:

- **Margins:** Generous — at least 0.5" on all sides, 0.75" preferred. White space is a feature, not waste. Brad thinks white space looks expensive.
- **Text alignment:** Left-aligned or centered depending on the page. Never justified.
- **Copy position:** Varies by page (top, middle, bottom) but always with breathing room. The copy should never feel crowded.
- **Image position:** Varies by page. Images can bleed to edges on spread pages. Single pages keep images within margins.

## Phase Labels

Small integrated design elements — not standalone dividers.

```
────────────────────────
PHASE 1: ASSESSMENT
────────────────────────
```

- Sans-serif, uppercase, gold text
- Thin gold horizontal rules above and below
- Positioned in the top margin or top corner of the first page of each phase
- Small enough to not compete with the page content
- Appears on: Page 2 (Phase 1), Page 6 (Phase 2), Page 14 (Phase 3)

## Image Style Direction

All images generated via Midjourney (Nano Banana 2). The style should be consistent across all pages and match the outer box packaging aesthetic.

### Style Keywords for All Prompts
- Editorial illustration style
- Warm muted tones (not oversaturated)
- Off-white/cream paper background texture
- Polished but with character — not sterile corporate stock photography
- Slightly stylized/exaggerated — more editorial than photorealistic
- Consistent with the OG Manual's illustration quality but through Brad's CORPORATE aesthetic (clean lines, polished rendering vs. the OG's hand-drawn sketchy style)

### Brad's Appearance (Consistent Across All Images)
- White male, late 30s
- Purple satin shirt, unbuttoned to mid-chest
- Oversized gold rope chain — comically large, think 80s rapper
- Aviator sunglasses (always, even indoors)
- Gold signet pinky ring
- Big cheesy teeth-too-white smile (unless a specific page calls for a different expression)
- Late 30s body in a 22-year-old's wardrobe

### Style Reference Prompt Suffix
Add to the end of every Midjourney prompt:

> editorial illustration style, warm muted tones, off-white textured paper background, polished and slightly stylized, premium brand book aesthetic, clean lines --ar [aspect ratio] --s 250 --v 6.1

**Spacing notes:**
- "FROM THE DESK OF" and "BRAD STERLING" are tightly coupled (2pt space-after) — they read as one phrase
- "A Strategic Advantage System" and "VOLUME I — FIRST EDITION" sit below the title as a descriptor group
- Two gold rules bracket the title+descriptor group, creating a frame-within-a-frame
- Black margins should feel balanced on all four sides; bottom margin may be slightly larger than top
- The text block sits as a unified medallion floating in black space

### Page 2 — Arrival

### Page 3 — Your Cell

### Page 4 — The Block
- Off-white/cream background
- Hallway/office environment image
- Barcode tag graphic element (BLOCK C - 00247) in monospace

### Page 5 — The Instincts
- Off-white/cream background
- Image showing survival behaviors — defensive desk arrangement, territorial markers, hoarded supplies
- "Not the people. The instincts." — serif, centered, lots of white space around it. These two lines should feel like a gut punch. Big type. Breathing room.

### Pages 6-7 — The Experiment (Spread)
- Off-white/cream background across both pages
- Phase label "PHASE 2: DIAGNOSIS" on page 6
- Three vignettes across the spread: Brad planting pen (left), the aftermath (center), Brad's conclusion (right)
- Copy flows across the spread in serif
- "CUBICLE SHANK" in sans-serif bold when it first appears — visual emphasis on the product name

### Pages 8-9 — The Loadout (Spread)
- This is a FORMAT SUBVERSION page — should look different from the narrative pages
- Could use a darker background (charcoal or dark gray) to signal "tactical briefing"
- Items displayed as equipment diagrams with monospace labels and spec callouts
- Framing copy in serif at the top of the left page
- Labels (NERVE AGENT, PERIMETER DEFENSE, SHIELD, CUBICLE SHANK) in sans-serif bold, uppercase

### Pages 10-11 — The Ink (Spread)
- Off-white/cream background
- Left page: status symbols (suit, watch, haircut) shown cleanly — aspirational styling
- Right page: the contrast — tattoo flash art style images of the earned marks (chalk outline sandwich, paper airplane)
- Copy flows across the spread
- "You earned your ink." in serif, larger, emphasis

### Pages 12-13 — The Code (Spread)
- Off-white/cream background
- Could be formatted as a decoder card or translation guide — the FORMAT is the straight man
- Office phrases on the left in clean sans-serif (how they sound)
- Translations on the right in serif (what they mean)
- "I just put it in writing." as the closer — serif, bottom right

### Pages 14-15 — The Reveal (Spread)
- **Dark background** (charcoal or matte black) — signals the finale. Visual contrast with all previous pages.
- Page 14: Brad's full portrait. Gold frame border. PHOTO COURTESY OF ELEVATE CO. in tiny gold text. Brad holding/presenting the open briefcase.
- Page 15: "Everybody's trying to survive. That's the problem. Nobody's trying to win." — white or gold serif on dark background. Big type. Maximum white space. This quote should feel like it's carved in stone.

## Production Notes

- **Page count:** 16 pages = 4 sheets, nested and saddle-stitched (2 staples on spine)
- **Orientation:** Portrait (~5.5" x 7" — confirm with sourcing)
- **Cover stock:** Heavier weight, matte coated, matte black + gold foil
- **Interior stock:** Matte coated text weight, off-white/cream
- **Color:** Full color throughout (CMYK + gold foil on cover)
- **The Loadout spread (8-9)** and **The Reveal spread (14-15)** use darker backgrounds — confirm ink coverage is printable at these page positions
