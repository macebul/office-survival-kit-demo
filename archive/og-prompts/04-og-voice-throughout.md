# Creative Session: OG Voice Throughout the Kit

## Context

The **Office Survival Kit** has two narrative layers: Brad Sterling's polished Elevate Co. branding (exterior, item boxes, formal materials) and The OG's hidden voice (sticky notes, hidden messages, smuggled text). This session focuses on developing a comprehensive **OG copy map** — every place his voice appears, what it says, and how the breadcrumb trail builds.

### OG's Voice Rules
- Dry, deadpan, institutional, weary amusement
- Short sentences. Says in 5 words what Brad says in 50.
- Never uses exclamation marks or emoji
- Never sounds angry — just wearily amused
- Refers to Brad as "he/him" — never by name
- Feels handwritten even when printed
- Prison-office vocabulary: yard, cell, block, inmate, issued, contraband, parole

### The Emotional Arc
The recipient discovers OG's voice gradually:
1. **First hint:** A sticky note on an item box ("He called this an 'Executive Tactical Stylus.' I can't.")
2. **Pattern recognition:** More notes on other boxes — "wait, who's writing these?"
3. **Deepening:** Hidden messages on tray walls and undersides
4. **Full voice:** Doc Set 3 / Manual 3 — first extended OG writing
5. **Finale:** The mug card — "You looked inside. Smart."

The goal: by the time you find the mug, OG feels like a real person you've been getting to know.

## Deliverables

1. **Copy for every location** in the placement map above (exact text, ready to print)
2. **A breadcrumb trail guide** — which notes connect to each other, what the discovery order suggests, and how OG's personality unfolds
3. **3 "best of" picks** — the notes you think will get photographed and shared on social media
4. **Kevin continuity check** — Kevin appears 4–6 times across the kit. Flag where he should show up in OG's notes (he should appear naturally, not forced)

## Constraints
- Sticky notes: under 15 words (ideally under 10)
- Hidden messages: under 25 words
- OG never uses exclamation marks or emoji
- Every note should be funny on its own but funnier in context of the whole trail
- The overall arc: stranger → familiar presence → someone you wish you could meet
