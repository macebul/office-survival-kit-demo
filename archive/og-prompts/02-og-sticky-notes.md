# Creative Session: OG Sticky Notes & Hidden Copy

## Context

You are writing copy for **The OG (Owen Granger)** — the original, anonymous creator of the Office Survival Kit. His voice appears throughout the product as handwritten-style sticky notes and hidden messages, discovered gradually during unboxing. He is the emotional backbone of the product.

**OG's voice:** Dry, deadpan, institutional, weary amusement. Short sentences. Never explains too much. Says in 5 words what Brad Sterling says in 50. He sounds like someone who has been in the office long enough to have wisdom, but not long enough to have lost his sense of humor about it.

**The other character:** Brad Sterling (B.S.) — Founder & Chief Vision Officer, Elevate Co. He found OG's underground kit, mass-produced it, and rebranded everything. Brad's polished marketing is everywhere on the exterior. OG's notes are the cracks in that facade — discovered inside, hidden, smuggled.

**World rules:** The office IS a prison. Not a metaphor. Vocabulary: yard, cell, block, inmate, issued, contraband, commissary, parole, sentence, intake, release.

**Comedy rule:** OG's notes should feel discovered, not presented. They're short, cryptic, and reward the person who finds them.

---

## What I Need

### 1. Item Box Sticky Notes (6 notes — one per item box)

Each tray item ships in an individual retail box. On or inside each box, there's a printed OG sticky note. These are the recipient's first "wait, who wrote this?" moment.

**Already drafted (refine or replace if you have something better):**
- **Cubicle Shank (pen):** "He called this an 'Executive Tactical Stylus.' I can't."
- **Lifer Ink (tattoos):** "You don't choose these. See the guide."
- **Turf Tags (stickers):** "Expand slowly. See rules inside."

**Need new notes for:**
- **Cell Talk:** (Brad calls them "Self-Actualization Protocol™ Cards")
- **Rap Sheets:** (Brad calls them "Interpersonal Feedback Delivery System™")
- **Sweet Nothings:** (Brad calls them "Ambient Communication Cards™")

**Tone guidance:** Each note should do one of these:
- Comment on Brad's ridiculous rebranding
- Give a cryptic usage tip
- Hint at OG's backstory
- Break the fourth wall slightly (talking to the recipient as if they're a new inmate)

### 2. Confiscated Stapler Note (1 note)

The stapler recess in Tray 2 is empty — just a chalk outline. The label reads: "CONFISCATED — WEAPONIZED OFFICE EQUIPMENT. INCIDENT #147. DO NOT REISSUE."

Write an OG sticky note for this recess. It should:
- Be short (under 10 words ideal)
- Reference the incident without explaining it
- Leave the recipient wanting to know what happened
- Draft directions to consider: "He needed it." / "Don't ask." / "Not since the incident."

### 3. Hidden Tray Messages (4–6 messages)

These appear on the inside walls of the trays (visible after items are removed) and on tray undersides. They reward the person who explores thoroughly.

**Locations:**
- Tray 1 inside walls (2 messages — left wall, right wall)
- Tray 1 underside (1 message — seen when tray is lifted)
- Tray 2 inside walls (1–2 messages)
- Tray 2 underside (1 message)

**Tone:** More personal than the item notes. These are OG talking to the recipient directly, now that they've gone deep enough into the kit. Mix of:
- Survival advice ("Trust no one with your lunch. Especially on Fridays.")
- Acknowledgment ("You found this. Good. That means you're paying attention.")
- Cryptic warnings ("The printer knows what you did.")
- Wry commentary ("Brad put his face on the box. I put mine in the margins.")

### 4. Briefcase Interior Hidden Messages (2–3 messages)

Small OG notes in unexpected places inside the briefcase itself:
- Under the compartment cover (seen after lifting it off)
- On the divider wall between front and back zones
- Inside the lid somewhere (not the main lid print — a separate small note)

These should feel like the deepest layer — the messages you only find if you really look.

### 5. Mug Card (1 message — already drafted, refine if needed)

A small printed card found inside the enamel mug (the finale reveal item):
**Current draft:** "You looked inside. Smart. That instinct will keep you alive in here."

Refine or provide 2–3 alternatives.

## Deliverables

For each section, provide:
1. The copy (exact text for each note/message)
2. A brief note on placement (where exactly it should go)
3. Flag any that you think are especially strong or that connect to other notes (building a breadcrumb trail)

## Constraints
- Each sticky note: **under 15 words** (ideally under 10)
- Each hidden message: **under 25 words**
- OG never uses exclamation marks
- OG never uses emoji
- OG never sounds angry — just wearily amused
- OG refers to Brad in third person ("he," "him") — never by name on the notes
- These should feel handwritten, not printed — short, lowercase-feeling even if capitalized
