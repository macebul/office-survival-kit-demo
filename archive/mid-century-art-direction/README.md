## Why This Was Replaced

The mid-century modern illustration style (angular geometric faces, flat color fills, limited palette, retro advertising poster feel) was replaced with a photorealistic direction. The new direction uses real-looking people and environments to create a straight-faced visual container that makes the absurd content hit harder through juxtaposition — a photorealistic image of someone in a hazmat suit responding to a ballpoint pen is funnier than a cartoon of the same scene because the viewer's brain processes it as plausible before the joke lands.
