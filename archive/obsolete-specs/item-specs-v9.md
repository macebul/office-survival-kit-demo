### Packaging
- **No individual box** — the mug is the finale reveal, discovered unwrapped in shredded classified paper. The raw reveal is more dramatic than a boxed presentation.

### Reference Notes
The joke: it's your mug shot. The name is the joke and the mug is the payoff. This is the finale item — the most expensive piece in the kit, the highest perceived value, the thing that makes people feel the kit was worth it. Needs to feel premium, not disposable. Hidden in the back-zone compartment behind the compartment cover's vertical surface — completely invisible until Tray 2 is lifted and the compartment cover is removed.

### Questions for Sourcing
- Enamel vs. stainless steel — cost difference?
- Single-side vs. wrap print — cost difference?
- Can we get 2-3 samples with different print methods?
- What's the realistic per-unit cost at 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 units?
- Confirm exact outer diameter and height — these drive the entire box depth dimension.

### Hidden Note (Easter Egg)
A small printed card or tag placed inside the mug: "You looked inside. Smart. That instinct will keep you alive in here." This is the final easter egg — discovered only by the curious. Near-zero COGS (single small card). Copy lives in Copy Document.

### COGS Alternatives (if enamel exceeds budget)
- **Option A:** Stainless steel camp mug — estimated $2-3 vs. $4-6 for enamel. Same joke, same size. Less premium feel but significant COGS savings.
- **Option B:** Smaller 8oz mug instead of 11oz — reduces mug dimensions, which cascades to smaller compartment, potentially smaller box, lower shipping/FBA costs.
- Primary spec remains 11oz enamel. Alternatives to be explored only if enamel COGS makes $10 target unachievable.
