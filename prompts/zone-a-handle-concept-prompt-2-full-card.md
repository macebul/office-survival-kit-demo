## Prompt

A full view of an L-shaped cardboard blister card backing, approximately 13.25 inches wide by 10.75 inches tall. The upper-left corner has a rectangular notch cut out (4.75" wide x 3.75" tall), creating the distinctive L-shape. This is 18pt white/cream heavy cardstock, printed full color on the front face.

The card is divided into three visible zones:

**LEFT COLUMN — Zone A "The Handle" (4.75" wide x 7.00" tall, below the notch):**
- A narrow vertical rounded-rectangle die-cut hole is punched through the left side of this strip (~1 inch wide cutout). This is a physical handle opening — you can see through it.
- To the right of the handle cutout, printed content:
  - **"BONUS"** header at the top — bold, colorful, eye-catching. Bright color block or starburst background (yellow, orange, or red). Large bold stencil/block lettering. This POPS — it's the most energetic visual element on the entire card.
  - **"SHIELD"** in clean institutional block text below the BONUS header.
  - Three simple stick-figure instructional diagrams stacked vertically showing how to use the strip as a shield. IKEA/airline safety card style — clean black line art, deadpan.
    - Figure grips the handle FROM THE LEFT SIDE (fingers through the die-cut opening), holds card vertically as a shield.
    - Figure deflects incoming objects (paper airplane, crumpled memo).
    - Figure stands protected behind the cardboard shield.
  - **CRITICAL: The figure holds the card from the LEFT SIDE where the handle is — NOT from the center.**
  - Very short step labels only (2-4 words each). Images do the work. NO fine print or disclaimer.

**RIGHT SIDE, LOWER — Zone B "The Product Zone" (8.50" wide x 7.00" tall):**
- This is the main product area. Show it with generic/placeholder product packaging design — a product name in large bold text (e.g., "CUBICLE SHANK" or similar), a clear area where a plastic blister would be glued (outline it or show it as a slightly different tone/border to indicate the blister footprint), and institutional branding elements (barcode area, regulatory marks, a tagline line).
- Professional retail packaging design. Dark background (matte black or very dark charcoal) with white and red typography. Clean, premium, institutional.

**RIGHT SIDE, UPPER — Zone C "The Upper Extension" (8.50" wide x 3.75" tall, to the right of the notch):**
- Upper branding area. Show a brand mark area (e.g., "ELEVATE CO." in small clean text), a division name, and a division tagline. Minimal, institutional.
- This zone visually continues from Zone B below it — same background color, feels like one continuous surface.

**The notch (upper-left, 4.75" x 3.75"):**
- This is empty space — no cardboard here. The surface underneath is visible through this cutout. This is where a product box (mug box) would sit when the cards are stacked in the briefcase.

**Overall composition:**
- The card is lying flat on a neutral surface (light gray desk, white surface, or kraft paper). Photographed from directly above or at a very slight angle.
- Natural, clean lighting. Product photography style.
- The L-shape should be clearly visible — the notch is obviously missing from the upper-left corner.
- The die-cut handle hole in Zone A is clearly visible — you can see through it to the surface below.
- The contrast between Zone A (white/cream background with colorful BONUS header and line-art diagrams) and Zones B/C (dark, premium, institutional product packaging) should be visible — Zone A has a different visual energy than the main product zone.
- No plastic blister is attached — this is just the printed cardboard backing viewed from the front.

**Do NOT include:** Plastic blister, actual products, retail peg display, other cards. This is ONE card, flat, front face up, showing the full L-shape with all three zones.

## Reference Notes for Design Team

- This image shows how Zone A (the handle strip) relates to the full card
- Zone A occupies the left column below the mug box notch — it's the natural grip area when pulling a card from the briefcase stack
- The die-cut handle replaces the traditional top-edge hanging hole
- The BONUS header is the visual bridge — it gives Zone A energy and purpose so the L-shape looks deliberate, not accidental
- Zone B + Zone C together form the "main card" that would exist without the handle strip
- On the back of the card, Zones B + C merge into one large unobstructed rectangle (8.50" x 10.75") of dense comedy content
- The 7 cards in the kit each have a different BONUS alternate use — this shows Shield as the reference
- Physical dimensions: 13.25" x 10.75" overall, 4.75" x 3.75" notch upper-left
