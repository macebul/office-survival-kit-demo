### How Brad's Comedy Works

Brad has ONE voice that operates across three situations with different levels of self-awareness:

| Brad is talking about... | Self-awareness | Comedy type |
|--------------------------|---------------|-------------|
| Office life / the product | High — he knows what's funny | Genuinely good jokes in straight-faced formats |
| Himself / his brand | Zero — complete delusion | Gap between self-image and reality |
| How he built the business | Negative — confesses without knowing it | Accidental honesty framed as achievement |

**Product Copy Brad** is actually competent. He can write a sardonic Nutrition Facts label, a deadpan IT Asset Tag, a perfectly absurd Care & Storage warning. The product humor works. This is why Elevate Co. exists.

**Personal Brand Brad** is a disaster. The moment the subject is himself — his story, vision, credentials, photo — every ounce of self-awareness evaporates. Hustle-culture LinkedIn post given physical form.

**THE KEY:** Don't sabotage the product copy to make Brad look dumber. The BETTER his product copy is, the funnier his personal brand becomes by contrast.
