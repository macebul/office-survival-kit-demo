# Companion Line Options — Round 4

The structure: "Because asking nicely would require [absurd thing that reveals how far gone you already are]"

The joke: asking nicely isn't just ineffective — it's impossible given what you've already done.

1. "Because Asking Nicely Requires Unclenching."

2. "Because Asking Nicely Means Giving Back the Chair."

3. "Because Asking Nicely Requires Eye Contact. And Yours Says Enough."

4. "Because Asking Nicely Would Mean Removing the Barricade."

5. "Because Asking Nicely Means Admitting You Took the Stapler."
