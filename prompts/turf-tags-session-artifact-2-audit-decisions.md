## COMEDY AUDIT RESULTS (Surface by Surface)

### FRONT FACE (5.0" x 3.5")

**What's locked/keeping:**
- TURF TAGS™ (product name)
- Dog silhouette (peeing on office chair) — strongest visual element
- Yellow/black palette (caution tape / warning sign vocabulary)
- "Workspace Personalization Labels" (Brad's rebrand)

**What needs work:**
- "MARK YOUR TERRITORY" — stays as the bold tagline, but it's descriptive, not comedic. Needs a companion line underneath that adds the actual comedy mechanism. The companion line is where the joke lives.
- "Because Asking Nicely Didn't Work" (current companion line) — right voice and tone, sardonic and warm, sounds like a person talking. But low comedy density: one read, one smile, nothing new on re-read. No subversion of expectations. The user wants this slot to surprise the reader — not just escalate logically but jump somewhere unexpected.
- Division name: needs to be decided — "Territorial Compliance Division" (v01) vs "Bureau of Territorial Affairs" (box copy doc)
- The v01 fine print ("*Anything that holds still long enough") is funnier and more compact than the box copy doc's replacement footnote system
- Evidence line from box copy doc ("This product has been used as evidence in 3 workplace disputes and 1 divorce") is strong — Recognizable + Cruel + Clever. Worth adding.
- Box copy doc's deployment notice, dual footnotes, and product code (EC-WPL-001) are overengineered and should be cut

**CRITICAL NOTE on companion line development:**
Multiple attempts were made to write a companion line under "MARK YOUR TERRITORY." All failed. The failures had these patterns:
- Too self-aware / too wordy / explaining the joke
- Too dry without absurdity
- Logical escalation instead of subverting expectations (reader expects "escalation after nicely" and kept getting escalation)
- Not funny — no surprise, no twist, no second layer

The user's guidance:
- Keep the voice of "Because Asking Nicely Didn't Work" — simple, flat delivery, sardonic, like talking to a friend
- But add a comedy MECHANISM — subvert expectations, go somewhere the reader doesn't see coming
- The line that came closest was "Because Asking Nicely Didn't Work. And Neither Did Asking Loudly." — right voice but too generic and predictable
- The user wanted to push "asking loudly" toward something more absurd
- Do NOT generate options that: are too dry, resolve logically, or let the reader predict the ending

**Next session should:** Either workshop this line collaboratively with the user, or present 3-5 options that genuinely subvert expectations (not logical escalation).
