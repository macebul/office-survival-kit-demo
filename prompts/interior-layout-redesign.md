# Interior Layout Redesign: Office Survival Kit

## What You're Solving

The Office Survival Kit is a comedy gift product packaged in a briefcase-shaped box. The interior layout is being completely redesigned. The previous version used Pelican-style die-cut trays with individually boxed items sitting in shaped recesses. The new version uses **carded blister packs** for most items and **stacking** instead of trays.

**Your job:** Figure out how all 7 items (each in its own retail-style packaging) physically fit inside the briefcase. The output is exact dimensions for every package, a stacking plan, and confirmation that everything fits with acceptable tolerances.

This must be solved BEFORE the graphic designer can start work. The designer needs exact surface dimensions for every package to design the layouts.

## What You Need to Figure Out

### 1. Blister Pack Dimensions

For each of the 6 blister-packed items, determine:
- **Card backing dimensions** (width x height) -- this is the primary design surface
- **Blister depth** (how far the plastic bubble protrudes from the card)
- **Overall package thickness** (card + blister)

Consider:
- Blister packs for flat items (tattoos, stickers) will be thinner than blister packs for the pen or card deck boxes
- The card backing should be as large as practical to maximize copy space -- the more cardboard real estate, the more comedy can fit
- Standard blister pack conventions: the card is usually significantly larger than the item (think of how a pen is blister-packed at a store -- the card is much bigger than the pen)
- There's a hanging hole at the top of most blister packs (for retail pegs). **Include the hanging hole** -- it adds to the authenticity of the parody, even though these won't actually hang on pegs.

### 2. Card Box Dimensions (for the 3 card decks)

Each card deck needs a small box before going in the blister:
- Card dimensions: ~3.5" x 2.5" (standard business card size, roughly)
- Stack height: ~0.5" for ~15 cards
- Box should accommodate the cards with a small tolerance
- Standard tuck-end box with a tuck flap closure

### 3. Mug Box Dimensions

- Standard gift mug box with a die-cut window on one face
- Must hold an 11oz enamel camp mug (~3.5" dia x 3.5" tall, 4.0" wide with handle)
- The box is printed on all 6 sides

### 4. The Stacking Plan

How do all 7 packaged items stack inside the briefcase?

**Interior space:** ~13.5" x ~11.0" x ~4.5"

Consider:
- Items should be accessible -- the recipient opens the briefcase and sees items arranged attractively
- The stacking should feel intentional, not just thrown in
- The mug box is the largest and heaviest item -- it probably goes on the bottom
- Blister packs could lay flat (card backing parallel to briefcase base) or stand up
- Multiple items can share a layer if they're the same thickness
- There should be minimal wasted space, but items don't need to fill every inch
- Think about what the recipient sees first when they open the lid -- that's the comedy's first impression

### 5. Interior Bottom Treatment

There may be a final joke or Easter egg printed on the interior bottom of the briefcase. This is only visible after ALL items are removed. The product owner hasn't written this copy yet but wants to reserve the option. Note whether the bottom will be fully visible or partially covered by the last item's packaging.

### 6. Confirm Overall Fit

- Do all items fit within ~13.5" x ~11.0" x ~4.5"?
- What is the total stack height? How much clearance is there between the top item and the lid?
- Is there enough room that items aren't jammed in, but not so much that they rattle around?
- If items don't fit, what changes? (Briefcase depth increase? Smaller blister cards? Different arrangement?)

## Constraints

- **The briefcase exterior dimensions should stay close to 14" x 11.5" x 5.0"** if possible. These are working estimates but changing them cascades to the outer Amazon shipping box dimensions and FBA size tier compliance. The Amazon box needs to fit within Large Standard tier (max 18" x 14" x 8").
- **Maximize the blister pack card size.** More cardboard = more copy space = more comedy. Don't shrink the cards unless you have to for fit.
- **Each item must be individually removable.** The recipient should be able to pull out one item at a time.
- **The mug is the heaviest item.** Account for this in the stacking plan (it shouldn't be on top of fragile items).
- **The briefcase will ship inside an outer Amazon box** with ~0.75" clearance per side and void fill. The briefcase itself doesn't need to survive a drop test alone, but items shouldn't shift significantly inside it during shipping.

## Reference: What Retail Blister Packs Look Like

For authenticity, the blister packs should follow real retail conventions:

- **Pen blister packs** (hardware/office supply stores): Card is usually ~4" wide x ~9-11" tall. Pen sits in a narrow blister in the lower 60% of the card. Upper portion is branding and copy. Hanging hole at top.
- **Card game / card deck blister packs** (bookstores, gift shops): Card is usually ~5-6" wide x ~7-9" tall. Card box sits in a deeper blister (needs to accommodate ~0.75" box depth). More square proportions.
- **Flat items (stickers, tattoos)**: Card is usually similar to the item size but with a header area for branding. Blister is very shallow (just enough to hold the flat sheet).

These are starting points. The actual dimensions should be determined by what fits in the briefcase while maximizing card surface area.

---

## Important: This Is a Comedy Product

The items inside are real (real pen, real tattoos, real stickers, real cards, real mug) but the packaging is a comedy experience. The blister packs are designed to look like authentic retail products -- someone should pick up the pen blister pack and think it's a real product for 2-3 seconds before they start reading the copy and realize it's hilarious.

This means the packaging dimensions should feel natural and proportional for each item type. A pen blister pack that's comically oversized or undersized breaks the illusion. Match the proportions of real retail packaging in each category.
