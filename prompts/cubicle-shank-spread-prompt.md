# Office Survival Kit — Cubicle Shank Magazine Ad Spread

## What This Is

I'm working on a premium gag-gift product called the **Office Survival Kit**. One of the pieces inside is a magazine called **ELEVATE** — a fake professional magazine "published" by Brad Sterling, the fictional founder of "Elevate Co. Workplace Solutions Division." The magazine is 8.5x11" portrait, full color, glossy, 30ish pages. It looks and feels like a real magazine.

I need you to concept, write, and generate Midjourney image prompts for **Pages 10-11** — a full 2-page spread that is a **product advertisement** for the **Cubicle Shank** (a tactical pen that is one of the items in the kit).

## The World Rule

**The office is a prison. This is not a metaphor.**

Nobody thinks it's clever to compare the office to prison. The comparison doesn't exist because there's nothing to compare — the office just IS this way. You serve your sentence. You do your time. You survive your shift. The language of incarceration IS the language of corporate life. It's the water everyone swims in.

## The Voice: Brad Sterling (B.S.)

**Brad Sterling** — Chief Vision Officer & Founder, **Elevate Co.** Workplace Solutions Division. A hustle-culture entrepreneur who mass-produced the Office Survival Kit and sells it on Amazon. His initials spell B.S. — he doesn't know.

### NEW Creative Direction (CRITICAL)

The booklet's voice has shifted. The old direction was deadpan, dry, institutional — nobody notices the horror. **That's retired.**

The new direction:
- **Professional magazine advertising aesthetic** — vibrant, colorful, well-staged
- **Corporate upbeat voice but content is insane** — the format is enthusiastic and optimistic about horrifying things
- **Marketing language** — fun, playful, persuasive, aspirational
- **Real estate imaging quality** — beautiful staging, aspirational photography
- **Fallout game commercial energy** — selling nuclear bunkers with 1950s optimism
- **More optimistic and silly, playfully funny**
- **Fun, well-staged scenes, photo magazine quality**
- **Russell Brunson sales funnel energy** — the persuasion structure is real, the product is insane
- **LOL or visual interest on every single page**
- **Color, vibrant**

The comedy engine is: **everyone is aggressively enthusiastic about the horror.** The format isn't oblivious — it's a willing, polished, professional participant. It's SELLING you the insanity with a smile, beautiful photography, and compelling copy.

Think:
- Fallout shelter ads (nuclear apocalypse sold with 1950s optimism)
- SkyMall catalog (absurd products presented with total sincerity)
- Russell Brunson sales pages (testimonials, urgency, transformation)
- Real estate staging (beautiful lighting, aspirational framing)
- QVC infomercial (the host genuinely believes this product will change your life)

### What the Voice Should NOT Be
- Not deadpan, dry, noir, or cool/detached
- Not "raised eyebrow" energy — it's "grabbing you by the shoulders" energy
- Not winking at the audience — the enthusiasm is genuine
- Not puns (too cute)
- The copy never says "get it?" — it just IS a real ad for something insane

## What I Need

### 1. Concept the Ad Spread
Two pages (left and right), 8.5x11" each. This should look like a **real product advertisement in a professional magazine.** The reader's brain should register "this is an ad" before it registers "wait, what is this ad FOR?"

Use advertising formats: hero product photography, aspirational lifestyle imagery, testimonials, before/after, pull quotes, spec tables, CTAs — whatever combination makes the comedy land hardest. Mix voice styles (QVC, wellness, transformation) within the spread if it serves the comedy.

### 2. Write the Ad Copy
In the new upbeat/enthusiastic voice. The copy should be:
- Light (15-50 words per page, not counting testimonials/specs)
- Playful, funny, persuasive
- Indistinguishable from a real product ad in FORMAT
- Insane in CONTENT
- The kind of thing that makes someone laugh out loud and then show it to someone else

### 3. Generate Midjourney Image Prompts
For the ad photography. These should produce vibrant, well-staged, professional product/lifestyle photography. NOT deadpan stock photos. Magazine ad quality.

**Midjourney prompt format:**
- 3x3 grid structure: [scene description] [character descriptions] [environment] [style anchor] [negative prompts] [trailing params]

**Style anchor for new direction:**
Professional magazine advertisement photography, vibrant color, aspirational staging, product photography quality, lifestyle imagery, well-lit, editorial quality.

**Negative prompts (ALWAYS include):**
```
--no cartoon, illustration, drawing, painting, sketch, anime, manga, chibi, stylized, flat color, cel shading, vector art, mid-century modern, geometric faces, angular faces, pointed noses, exaggerated proportions, caricature, waxy skin, plastic skin, airbrushed skin, unnaturally smooth skin, symmetrical face, perfect features, model-beautiful, glamour lighting, fashion photography, Instagram filter, color grading, teal and orange, desaturated, oversaturated, HDR, lens flare, vignette, film grain for style, collage, composite, split screen, floating elements, text overlay, watermark, stock photo watermark, Getty Images watermark
```

**Trailing params:**
```
--ar [as needed] --s 250 --v 6.1
```
